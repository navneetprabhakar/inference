LIMITS THAT MATTER
- The trained context is 2,048 tokens, including history and reply reservation.
  Newly generated 3K–4K data has not been trained. Reset when context is exhausted.
- Tools only calculate and convert supported length, mass, volume and time units. They do not run code,
  access files, browse, send messages or perform physical actions.
- Retained step605 preserves18/18 exposed tool regression cases, but root review
  found only6/180 passing turns and0/60 complete sessions on a broader synthetic
  assessment. Treat this as an experimental model, not a reliable assistant.
