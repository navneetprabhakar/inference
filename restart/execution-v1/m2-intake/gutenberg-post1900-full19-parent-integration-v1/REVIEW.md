# New Gutenberg development cohort

- All 19 complete main units (602,810 bytes) and all ten linked notes were read in full. Review coverage is disjoint and bound to exact source hashes.
- Main units contain 199,719 provisional M1 tokens. Seventeen pass the unchanged English filter, leaving 173,812 provisional tokens. Units 21250 and 15447 fail because of retained foreign-language passages; no quotation stripping or threshold change was applied.
- Ten notes contain 1,679 tokens, reported separately. They are companions, not ten additional main documents.
- The units are readable and coherent. No major extraction corruption was identified in this fixed development set. This is not a fresh probability or token-weighted acceptance sample and establishes no source-wide pass rate.
- Concrete follow-ups include image/caption dependencies in 1762 and 19011; localized text errors in 23638, 66132 and 15447; and a factual contradiction in 23638. Original text remains unchanged.
- Historical prejudice, satire and fictional claims are recorded separately from readability. Conversation-training preferences do not create new base-prose rejection rules.
- No documents have been admitted. Source components, rights, current leakage checks and family/split custody still require disposition. These results do not establish that broad English supply is sufficient.
