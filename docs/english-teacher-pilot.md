The separately authorized **20 additional calls are complete**. All requested **GPT-6.1 Sol** through the isolated CLI0.163.0 and saved ChatGPT login; no alternative model or Platform API was used. The older20-slot pilot remains unchanged, giving40historical-plus-current launch reservations. No additional calls remain authorized. The proposed200-call expansion was **not** authorized.

The pilot produced **182 final assistant continuations across20hand-authored template families**. The first two calls generated one continuation each and passed direct review before the remaining18calls generated ten each. All182responses passed schema, structured-oracle, length and protected-text checks. Fifty replies also passed independent exact-answer oracles.

Human attestation and training admission remain false.

**Do not train another small memorization iteration with this packet.** The180considered examples contain only **2,890supervised student-token targets, including end markers**, and12,266prepared prediction positions for one pass. This is a transport/quality/candidate-adapter pilot, not a replacement for the planned English foundation or10K-dialogue SFT population. No student gradients ran and the prior0/100student-conversation result remains unchanged.

## What the review establishes

- Grammar corrections, literal extraction, latest-owner corrections, inventory formatting and small supplied arithmetic produce the expected exact strings. The teacher was given independently authored oracles; this is not an unseen capability benchmark.
- Other reviewed examples preserve updated days, negation, named items, delivery dates, missing-information scope and relevant supplied facts. Professional/casual/terse/humorous/caveman rendering is directly considered per reply; structured style labels alone are not evidence.
- The two pronoun exclusions demonstrate why copied structured facts cannot verify free-text faithfulness. A schema-valid response may still add an unsupported assumption.
- Several families use nearly identical wording after substituting objects, names or quantities. All180considered answer strings are distinct, but that does **not** establish semantic diversity. Keep family grouping and exposure caps; do not count these as180independent skills or scale the same templates to meet a data target.
- This is one direct expert assessment of a deliberately simple, bounded training cohort. It is neither human-attested review nor a calibrated estimate of general teacher performance. Student benefit remains untested.

## Adapter correction and actual provenance checks

The initial frozen generator composed raw contexts plus teacher continuations without inserting the style metadata into student-visible messages. Some otherwise identical contexts therefore had differing styles with no corresponding input control. Those original v1records remain retained as evidence.

A separately versioned `english_continuations` adapter now adds the existing host contract, `Response style:… Content setting:sfw.`, as a system message. It leaves all supplied history and generated reply text unchanged. This follows the established host-bridge convention and avoids contradictory supervision from hidden style labels. A style-control receipt binds the added instruction; it is not falsely attributed to teacher-generated history.

All182candidate transcripts were reconstructed from the completed durable cache and **all20actual CLI captures**. The checker verifies the exact source prompt/schema, final response, allowed event stream, token counters, successful terminal receipt and captured-file hashes. Unknown actions/errors remain refused. The previous pilot journals and generation-source hashes remain unchanged.

The frozen16K BPE re-encodes every complete style-controlled conversation. Only the final generated assistant content and its termination markers are supervised using `last-user-turn-v1`; earlier assistant history, system controls and user turns remain input only. All candidates fit1,024positions. This is prepared exposure, not executed gradient exposure.

The final focused suite passes **43tests**, covering the prior CLI/Sol contracts plus batching, durable failure/cache behavior, protected-text exclusion, actual subprocess protocol, two-call review gate, fixed deadline/source guards, style-visible inputs, continuation masks and capture tampering. Full actual reconstruction also passes. These checks make no new CUDA or model-learning claim.

## Course correction after this pilot

1. **Return to the missing language-learning phase.** Finish scalable protected-family grouping, finite source admission and the executable learning/resource/useful-portability contract for the existing acquired prose population. Keep the prior2Mpreliminary/78Mlanguage/20MconditionalSFT reservation and USD50/oneL4/45VM-hour limits. No new source acquisition or more tiny SFT loops are justified by this teacher pilot.
2. Treat these180considerations as small candidate ingredients. Complete applicable source/account evidence and a finite teacher-admission policy before any training release. Broader public conversation data supplies the required breadth; family-disjoint final evaluations remain protected.
3. **Do not scale the current echo-heavy schema unchanged.** Future teacher output should primarily contain useful assistant language, with facts/state checked independently rather than copied into every output. Broaden actual situations and conversation trajectories before adding parameter variants; retain source provenance and explicit style controls. Such a future recipe needs its own frozen validation and authorized calls.
4. After the base-language gate, run balanced, sufficiently broad conversation tuning and measure raw student generations. Report student task success separately from teacher quality and pipeline completion. No model improvement is claimed by this milestone.

The complete conversation/styles/content/tool/MCP/portability/ESCI objective remains unfinished. No further teacher generation follows automatically from completion of the20-call stage.
