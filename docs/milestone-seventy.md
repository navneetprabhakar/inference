## Finite policy and boundaries

The policy binds the exact staged origin, screen report, both review journals, review report, source/card/license evidence, cached Common Crawl terms and retained membership. No automatic source expansion. Original canonical text, split groups and partitions remain exact. All observed nonapprovals stay out; no body-only extraction, repaired text or new derivative is silently substituted.

The source decision accepts the pinned ODC-BY database evidence for a finite private offline experiment, preserving notices, attribution, original URLs and crawl provenance. Underlying document rights, facts, downstream licensing and public redistribution are not certified. The policy explicitly records substantial residual factual, grammar, spam, privacy and content risks among the **758 unread training records**; the sample results do not disappear behind a generic approval label.

Ceilings, not a recipe or launch permission:

- Local MLX or PyTorch CPU only; no cloud training under this admission.
- At most **2,000,000 physical data positions /4,096 updates /two physical passes per group**, including discarded real-data work.
- At most 100M parameters /2,048 context, with no external pretrained student initialization.
- Exact current membership only; any broader intake requires a new explicit admission artifact.

Before gradients, require a checked schema-6 research publisher and independent reader, full token/position/mask reconstruction, global heldout/request isolation, durable physical exposure accounting, explicit own-checkpoint optimizer transition/resume, and frozen learning/retention/resource/conversation contracts. This small pool alone is insufficient for the proposed main-learning experiment. It is also not authorization to reset an old run's accounting or repeat a control until a metric passes.
