## Decision

Run the prepared, separately bounded conversation/tool SFT diagnostic next after hardware preflight. It is the shortest direct test of the requested interface, not evidence that the base is ready. A failure to learn useful conversation must trigger a data/base intervention rather than endless SFT epochs or optimizer comparisons. Preserve the frozen screen selection (`preserve-095`); do not relabel the lower-loss `reset-scaled` endpoint as its winner.

The observed results support only this narrow conclusion: changing beta2 improved next-token loss (4.3395 versus 4.4891 at equal 20M branch exposure), but all endpoint strict-context scores are 1/32. The scaled branch also flags repetition in 32/32 completions versus control31/32. These are local results from `runs/optimizer-ablation-v1/comparison.json`, not a demonstration of improved assistant behavior. The three branches are alternative lineages; their 60M total compute is not 60M training on one candidate.

## What the literature does and does not support

- The official [SmolLM2-135M card](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M) reports 2T pretraining tokens. Its function-calling statement explicitly applies to the 1.7B model. Similar parameter count therefore supports architectural possibility, not comparable sample efficiency or tool competence at our exposure. Do not import its weights or use its results as our acceptance evidence.
- [LIMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11206) learns interaction from1,000 examples on a pretrained65B model. Its evidence concerns exposing existing pretrained capabilities; it does not show that a weak124M base acquires missing language competence from a small SFT set.
- [Instruction Pre-Training](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.14491v2), Tables1–2/14, finds benefits from augmenting raw documents with grounded instruction-response pairs at equal training-token budgets. Its smallest experiment is500M trained on100B tokens;200M generated pairs represent roughly10B tokens, not200M training tokens. This motivates a local hypothesis about data organization, but is not a feasible reproduction or a quantitative gain forecast here.
- [TinyStories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759) establishes that simplified synthetic language can yield coherent narratives in small models. Narrative coherence is not multi-turn factual recall or correct tool execution. Prior local `reports/smaller-own-base-countercheck.md` already records exposure/accounting limitations; a smaller restart remains unproven.
- Apple's [MLX compilation documentation](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx/blob/main/docs/src/usage/compile.rst) supports compiling full training graphs, warming compilation, and synchronizing actual work for measurement. Input-shape/type changes can recompile. It provides no M4 speedup guarantee for this full model or our variable-length SFT rows. Existing local parity and full-benchmark prerequisites remain necessary.

## Three bounded experiments, ordered by decision value

1. **Actual interface learning, now.** Freeze one full pass of each admitted source as the initial diagnostic candidate unless hardware evidence warrants a different prospective budget. Current loader accounting for that allocation:3,939,349 assistant targets,18,675,882 input positions and4,565 updates. These denominators differ; do not estimate training duration from assistant targets divided by pretraining throughput. Use preflight source/length timings plus checkpoint/evaluation overhead and slowdown margin. Evaluate the actual exported model on the frozen442sessions/632turns. Report relevant/consistent conversation, correct proposed/executed calls, observed-result readback, repetition and complete-session rates separately; manual judgments stay pending until read. One-pass allocation is a proposed ceiling for learning whether the interface is learnable, not a promise or acceptance relaxation.
2. **If syntax learns but grounding/recall does not: matched data intervention.** Prospectively cap a new pilot at1M assistant targets from short, source-grounded tasks: supplied facts, entity substitutions, corrections, changed tool observations and explicit no-call cases. Pair contrasting facts/observations while varying phrasing and names; keep family splits and full existing evaluation exclusions. Compare against an equal-update/equal-source baseline from the same own checkpoint; keep all prepared development cases out of training. Prefer verifiable supplied facts and executable arithmetic over unverifiable teacher knowledge. The1M cap is an engineering pilot choice, not a literature prescription. If neither cohort produces connected relevant language, stop this intervention and return to clean language pretraining rather than adding template repetitions.
3. **If substantially more training is required: benchmark compute before committing days.** Use the already prepared full-model Torch-versus-MLX protocol, with synchronized sustained timings, numerical parity, full optimizer resume, and real checkpoint overhead. Do not introduce a migration merely because a tiny fixture passes. Require the existing suggested25% sustained improvement criterion before migration work; otherwise retain Torch. Reassess the cleaner/simpler language curriculum only after an admitted corpus exists—the deferred30M web allocation remains unavailable. At1,400targets/s,1B positions alone takes about8.3days; this arithmetic is not a quality guarantee or an SFT forecast.

The scaled-beta2 endpoint is worth retaining as a possible future matched SFT comparator because its loss signal is real.
