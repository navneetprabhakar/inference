  • Contrastive numeric operands/operators and independently varied returned values: 1,098 paired result examples in training keep request/answer relationships explicit.
  • All five tools appear under canonical and renamed schemas, with descriptions and distractor tools.

Evaluation corrections

  • Report individual argument accuracy, each chain step, conditional second-step accuracy and full trajectory success.
  • After a wrong but executable call, use that call's actual local fixture result and continue; never inject the gold result for a wrong call.
  • Separate answer correctness conditional on exact calls from full task success. Dedicated result-only cases isolate copying of returned facts.
  • Preserve canonical exact matching. Add finite accepted reference variants for clarification; do not use keyword-only success.
  • No-tool answer correctness and abstention are separate. General chat quality remains pending blind human review; no automated metric is labelled human quality.
  • The new test split is locked by default in the evaluator. A future explicit unlock is required after development selection; 0 model evaluations on it so far.
  • V2's full final scores remain unchanged. The comparison includes paired outcome counts and offline argument diagnostics; missing steps from old early-stop runs are labelled unobserved.

Validation

28 tests passed. Independent audit checked all token/mask roundtrips, hashes, source receipts, budget accounting and coverage. 1,350 gold cases and 1,350 deliberately wrong answers verified the scorer. These are evaluator tests, not model accuracy results. No exact cross-split episode/family overlaps; no old held-out episodes added to training; replay blocks do not exactly match protected held-out loss blocks.

Limits

This remains a synthetic fixture benchmark with template-correlated cases, not a broad natural-language tool benchmark. Fresh chat received automated filtering, not human quality certification. Near-duplicate screening uses first-user text; it cannot prove absence of all semantic overlap. Finite accepted answers can reject valid paraphrases. The 120-answer blinded review packet is prepared but unrated.

Keep LR 3e-5 as the tool-repair research candidate and LR 1e-5 as the retention/grounding fallback. LR3's completed test gains in single-tool success are accompanied by clarification and retention regressions; neither dominates across all metrics.
