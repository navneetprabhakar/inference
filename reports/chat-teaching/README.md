# Conversation teaching pools

`chat_teaching.examples(seed)` builds a deterministic practice mixture covering correction, recall, extraction, rewrite, turn-to-turn instructions, evidence limits, clarification, and social conversation. At reference seed 713 it yields 3,379 unique histories, with 1–3 substantive user turns. Its frequency distribution is intentionally uneven because combinations of slots give some families more distinct examples. `manifest.json` reports the exact counts.

`chat_teaching.natural_examples(seed)` is a distinct sampling pool intended for 15–20% of training examples. It includes 241 histories (229 have a follow-up), up to 155 tokens in the project's versioned dialogue format. If the two pools are sampled together, 52 everyday exchanges are deliberately present in both: 24 short chats, 16 explanations and 12 OpenAssistant examples. These are training repetitions. Report their exposure without counting them as new distinct texts.

## Source and review

- **Original authored.** 146 hand-written two-turn situations are stored in `natural-turns.txt`. Each line contains the first user turn, its answer, the follow-up and its answer in that order. Their tasks include practical writing, explanations, creative help, reference, grounded extraction, handling incomplete evidence, asking for missing information, and accurately describing local capability limits. Another 40 original multi-turn conversations come from `SMALL_TALK` and `EXPLAIN`. The slot drills in the larger pool are generated separately. Authorship and content review were by the same assistant. There was no human or independent factual review.
- **Everyday Conversations.** The train_sft Parquet from HuggingFaceTB/everyday-conversations-llama3.1-2k revision `14f5432` has SHA-256 `4750214d12b4b92d07fca953e0b5cc59e313c575282d08b605c639aa87531daa`. The supplied card names Apache-2.0 and identifies Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct as the original generator. The 43 chosen rows are synthetic source material. The assistant read 81 two-exchange candidates and selected conversations with relevant answers to both turns. Exclusions included an invented employer shuttle, advice about personal health, outdated services, dubious science and ungrounded statements about famous people. `everyday-selected.jsonl` retains source row IDs and full topic paths. Each kept history is exactly `messages[2:6]` from `train_sft`, dropping the canned greeting and any third exchange. The separate source test partition was not accessed. [Source card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/everyday-conversations-llama3.1-2k/tree/14f5432)
- **OpenAssistant 2.** 12 reviewed short English exchanges from OpenAssistant/oasst2 revision `179dd21fc55192153d94adb0e0ce8f69e222bf75` are reproduced verbatim, with tree and assistant-reply IDs in `oasst-selected.jsonl`. The local decoded ready-tree JSONL has SHA-256 `93092c0f05695da0306d7ea78bfc10a25b00396a7b4625a2d18a1377fb0aae04`. The selection required both official and local training partitions, checked original message text and lineage, English, approved moderation, nondeleted, nonsynthetic nodes, and no positive PII, spam, language-mismatch, violence, hate-speech or sexual-content label. The selected pairs were reviewed for relevance, unsafe or unsupported recommendations and suitability for this local project. Archived bulk sample-only labels did not confer eligibility. Only the explicitly inspected rows were admitted. User IDs and timestamps were dropped. The source card specifies Apache-2.0; its license is retained in `OASST-LICENSE.txt`. [Source card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenAssistant/oasst2/tree/179dd21fc55192153d94adb0e0ce8f69e222bf75)

`natural-manifest.json` records file and runtime hashes. The runtime hash uses UTF-8 bytes of `json.dumps(natural_examples(713), sort_keys=True)`. Every natural history was checked against the GPT-2 tokenizer through `dialogue.training_row`. Different seeds shuffle the same natural records. Seeds change both the generated slot values and order in the larger pool.
