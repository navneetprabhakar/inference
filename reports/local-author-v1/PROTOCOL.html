Local author-only generation protocol

Authorized 31 October 2026: finish all 10,000 conversations before quality review or evaluation. No training or paid APIs.

  • 200 existing assistant-authored dialogues are copied with their provenance intact.
  • 9,800 new dialogues are generated by the local Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 4-bit teacher. This teacher is not the 97.5M student and does not replace its weights.
  • Zero curated pilot conversations are admitted. Scenario instructions vary across topics, settings, dialogue directions and seeds; responses are generated, not expanded from answer templates.
  • Mechanical checks only: JSON schema, alternating roles, complete teacher stop, whole-dialogue context fit and exact duplicate exclusion. No quality scores, semantic reviews, student inference or training occur.
  • Each attempt retains its prompt, seed and raw output; each accepted slot is saved atomically. Interrupted slots can be retried without losing completed ones.
  • Exactly 9,800 accepted local slots plus the existing 200 publish the final corpus. Failures do not silently reduce the target or pad it with duplicated records.
  • Continue on battery. Critical-thermal pause and resource limits remain active. Caffeinate prevents idle sleep, not lid-closure suspension.
  • All records remain candidates pending later review. Generated statements are not verified by successful formatting.

Live files

runs/local-author-v1/job.json: supervisor/download. status.json: generator progress after model load. console.log: raw process log. data/author-only-v1/accepted/: completed teacher records. attempts/: all saved attempts. Final corpus exists only after completion.

Stop and resume

Create runs/local-author-v1/STOP to stop at a checked boundary. Do not remove a user STOP without renewed consent. After inspecting a failure, the same supervisor command resumes verified download chunks and generation slots. Code/config/input/model hash changes block resumption. Never rerun the plan builder over frozen inputs.

runs/local-author-v1/env/bin/python scripts/run_local_author_job.py

The supervisor passes --resume to the generator when runtime state exists. Preserve the isolated environment and requirement lock. No old training STOP files are removed; this job has its own separate STOP.

Pinned model

MLX model repository; upstream model and license. Public model download only; generation runs with Hugging Face and Transformers offline mode.
