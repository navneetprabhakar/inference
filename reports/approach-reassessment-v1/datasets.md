# Dataset and curriculum reassessment — 2026-11-08

**Recommendation: test a broader, simple-language mixture; do not make 31M stories or another round of slot templates the entire recovery plan.** The cheapest new source to investigate is a bounded, audience-stratified Cosmopedia-v2 sample. For conversation, first reassess already downloaded Smol/Everyday material under a new prospective protocol: downloading the same dataset again adds nothing. No dataset was downloaded, admitted, packed or trained for this review. Narrative packing remains paused.

## What changes the decision

- The closest published 135M reference, SmolLM2, used **2 trillion pretraining tokens**, versus approximately122M in our lineage. Its success establishes capacity, not that a few million new targets will repair this checkpoint. [Official model card](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M)
- The authors explicitly shortened Smol-SmolTalk conversations for models below1B, reduced rewrite/summary emphasis, and **omitted function calling and advanced math**. This is evidence against copying a large-model instruction mix wholesale into our124M model. [Small-model dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/smol-smoltalk)
- Our narrower natural-language supply was partly caused by selection and split decisions, not lack of public data. Repeated errors justify rejecting defective examples; eight-example samples do not establish that every example in a source is unusable. Conversely, restoring a cohort wholesale would ignore real errors already observed.
- TinyStories supports narrow coherent story generation at very small scales. That result does not establish assistant instruction following, factual reliability, correction, or tool-result use. Our fresh32-story review found15retain/10review/7hold; the remaining pool is not certified clean. [TinyStories paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759)

## Critical correction: Everyday and Smol are not missing inputs

Read local `reports/sft-readiness-v3/QUALITY_REVIEW.md`, v4/v5 equivalents, `reports/scaled-teaching/README.md`, `reports/chat-teaching/README.md`, and current language inventory.

- V3 retained774Everyday training histories/96,343assistant targets after proposed individual deferrals, **zero validation under existing selection/splits/custody**. V4 fresh sample4retain/2review/2hold. V5 then deferred the entire Everyday, constraints and summary5k cohorts, not only bad rows.
- Earlier scaled teaching already contained230Everyday histories overall, including209creative-cohort examples. Chat teaching used43reviewed source rows, taking two substantive exchanges and removing greeting/third exchange. These are not untouched new data. Likewise v5 retained22,791Smol histories/3,993,989targets before final framing, mainly Magpie, summaries and rewrites. The original failed SFT already tested substantial natural instruction data.
- The **potentially different intervention** is a prospectively reviewed mix that restores useful ordinary-dialogue coverage and balances source tasks, while keeping complete relevant history and actual model capability claims. It is not “try SmolTalk” again. Preserve all known bad-ID deferrals, historical train/dev/protected exclusions and old receipts. Do not move held-out families into train to fill a quota.
- Audit why a semantically broad cohort has zero validation: a taxonomy grouping every short everyday dialogue as one family can make validation impossible, while random row splits can leak paraphrases. Neither is evidence of model generalization. A new source-design protocol can define meaningful task/state/topic groups and reserve *new eligible* groups prospectively; it cannot retroactively relabel protected examples or repair zero validation by resampling until favorable.
- Separate source-grounded tasks from unsupported factual advice and fictional roleplay. Judge a rewrite against its supplied text, not as the assistant claiming those events occurred. Conversely, an ungrounded invented local service or promised future action remains a bad assistant target.

## Concrete bounded curriculum proposal — hypothesis, not activation

1. **CPU intake pilot first:** at most1GiB new compressed data across fixed Cosmopedia shards,20GiB disk floor, fixed time limit. No bulk retrieval. Inspect a deterministic64document sample spanning audience/format/length and report pass reasons plus actual eligible GPT2 yield. Preserve historical/public custody and source-topic grouping. If useful clean text does not fit budget, stop; do not refill with progressively looser filters. This is a proposed budget, not a measured acquisition guarantee.
2. **Equal-budget language comparison, only after causal/backend checks:** compare2M narrative targets against2M targets of a proposed50%Cosmopedia simple expository/30%broad English prose/20%stories mix, same ownbase parent, optimizer and measured exposure. Broad prose may be a genuinely new FineWeb-Edu or DCLM lane only after intake clears; if unavailable, explicitly revise the arm to80%Cosmopedia/20%stories rather than pretending the web quota exists. Fractions are a diagnostic hypothesis, not an optimum from a paper. Measure held-out prose coherence and prompt sensitivity, not just source loss. At previous measured1,257–1,450targets/s,2Mtargets alone is roughly23–27minutes per arm; new backend/context requires remeasurement and evaluation adds time. No claim2M is sufficient for assistant competence.
3. **Small balanced SFT after a measurable language gain:** cap0.3–0.5M assistant targets, approximately50%short varied conversational/request-fulfillment examples,30%passage-grounded question/correction/recall,20%executed tool trajectories. Undershoot if audited natural coverage is inadequate; do not inflate by repeats. Use already-local eligible Everyday/Magpie/OASST material first, explicitly measuring novelty and source/task proportions. Hold parent/source-task groups out before rendering. Compare with the identical SFT budget on the uncontinued ownbase to isolate pretraining benefit. Existing full public gates remain unchanged.

Tool trajectory curriculum should progress exact copied values → one call with actual observation → correction/retry → two dependent calls and final grounded answer. Require every reference/value in the target to be present in user state or observed tool result, verify arithmetic with an independent executor, and vary natural wording/compositions beyond slots. Small synthetic state tasks are useful diagnostics but our failed grounded run already disproves treating finite-template correctness as conversational coverage.

Published APIGen/xLAM provides60K function-call examples and executable verification ideas, but its cited models are1B/7B; its schema shows query/tools/answers, not proof of our required multistep result-conditioned sessions. Files currently require accepting access conditions despite a CC-BY4.0 card. Use the methodology, not an assumed drop-in local solution. [Official dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/xlam-function-calling-60k). Toolformer similarly motivates teaching use of tool outputs; its result is not evidence that our weak124M checkpoint can self-generate useful training trajectories. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761)

**Stop condition:** if equal-budget language exposure does not improve unseen continuation/conditioning, do not pile on more template SFT. Report that the present compute/token budget has not established a usable model and revisit total learning budget or architecture/conditioning evidence with root. Access to billions of free tokens solves data availability; it does not solve local training time.
