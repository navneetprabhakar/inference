# Small-model conversation and tool training: reassessment

Research snapshot: 2026-11-08. Primary papers, author repositories and model cards; published scores below are author-reported, not independently reproduced here. No weights downloaded, private cases accessed, training launched, or benchmark activation frozen.

## Decision

- **Continue pursuing the own base, but stop treating another template SFT epoch as a credible remedy.** Sub-200M tool ability is plausible: there are direct scratch-model reports. None reviewed establishes reliable joint free conversation and tool execution within our measured M4 training budget.
- **Base capability and compute remain the first gates.** Our parent has approximately122.454M foundation tokens. Relevant scratch specialist reports use3.25–10B pretraining tokens, then substantial post-training. Those are examples, not a proven minimum or a promise that more tokens alone fix our model.
- **Keep the original architecture while measuring full-model throughput/parity.** Do not replace the student with pretrained weights, migrate architecture on the strength of a paper abstract, or begin RL before basic held-out call selection works.

TOAM is particularly relevant because its remaining faults include binding text to the wrong argument and emitting one call for a parallel request. Its improvements are a useful curriculum lead, not an executable reproduction recipe. Training on public synthetic teacher data is also distinct from initializing the student with pretrained weights; it preserves scratch weight ancestry, but teacher/data provenance and licenses still need review.

At the parent's current measured baseline of~8.12days per billion tokens,3.25B and10B alone imply~26.4 and81.2 continuous days respectively; Pico's11.215B implies~91.1days before RL. These are arithmetic illustrations using our124M throughput, **not estimates of those differently sized models' actual training cost**. A full-model MLX benchmark may change that materially; a tiny benchmark cannot.

## What our failure says, and what to change

Local evidence: [primary review](../grounded-dev-evaluation-v1/PRIMARY-TOOLS-REVIEW.md). Grounded SFT falls from92/422 to0/422 complete sessions after unchanged manual criteria; correct required-call sequences151/453→4/453. There are345 required turns without parsed calls, including257 protocol-valid responses. The stock clarification “What is the second operand?” appears91 times. This is not predominantly a JSON-format problem or exact-math scoring artifact.

1. **Measure and balance decisions, not only source tokens.** Current `own_base_sft_train.py:updates` batches each source separately and interleaves proportional to its update count. Each update normalizes summed masked CE by that update's assistant targets. Thus long answers do not simply dominate the whole run by raw target count. Chat has25,022/31,794 rows (~78.7%) versus~64.8% of assistant targets; with common batch size its update share is approximately79% (rounding depends on partial batches). The optimizer receives many more chat steps. Record exact updates, target tokens, call/no-call/ask decisions, source loss and gradient norms before proposing a mixture. A bounded matched-budget source-mixture ablation is justified; an optimal ratio is **not established**.
2. **Stage the task transitions while retaining language.** Continue broad-language learning only after throughput and held-out language-learning gates; then a bounded simple conversation/single-call stage; then call→actual result→answer; then clarification resolution, tool errors and multi-call dependencies. Keep matched language-retention checks. Existing SFT already masks to assistant targets and serializes full histories, so “turn on assistant-only loss” is not a new fix. Per-decision weights or separately normalized call/argument/readback losses are hypotheses requiring a small controlled test, not automatic production edits.
3. **Teach contrasts and evidence binding with natural diversity.** Fresh minimal pairs should change exactly the decision-relevant fact: complete vs missing value, permitted vs forbidden call, operation direction, signed decimal, literal identifier, unit direction. Every argument must bind to the request, earlier state or schema default; every follow-up must bind to the actual observation, including errors. Train error→correction and ask→user supplies value→call transitions. These examples must not be derived from the exposed failed prompts; use independent scenarios and family splits. More numeric substitutions of the same few sentences are insufficient diversity.
4. **Separate diagnostic levers from reported model capability.** Grammar-constrained decoding may reduce syntax failures, but cannot recover the257 valid no-call responses or wrong operations. Evaluate raw and constrained modes separately if added. Programmatic argument extraction or answer rendering would move capability into the runtime and must be disclosed. Do not quietly use either to claim the base learned tools. Avoid pointer-head/tokenizer/architecture changes now: they add confounds and lack task-specific evidence.
5. **Make the next gate causal and bounded.** Keep the same parent and total update budget when comparing a mixture or curriculum; use independent public development families, with no training on evaluation text. Track call selection, exact arguments, no-call precision, actual-observation readback, complete sessions and conversation retention alongside teacher-forced loss. Promote only meaningful generalization gains. A low loss or successful memorization test does not qualify a model as usable.

The justified next action is the full124M feasibility/parity measurement plus a data/decision-distribution audit. More complex synthetic chains, blind oversampling, RL, and architecture replacement remain speculative until the simpler capability gates pass. No source reviewed supplies a guaranteed zero-paid, short-duration recipe for our complete objective.

## Preserved implementation status

The MLX worker/controller work is paused for this reassessment. CPU worker controls previously passed5 groups, controller8 groups; no GPU benchmark executed and no activation spec frozen. Remaining integration review includes actual runtime-version/spec gates, full causal/parity evidence and fresh-process adapter continuation. Source checkpoints and frozen runtimes remain unchanged. Research findings do not authorize a benchmark launch or any new dataset admission.
