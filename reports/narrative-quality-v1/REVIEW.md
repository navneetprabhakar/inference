- **Useful material:** straightforward lost-object, sharing, clarification/dialogue and short causal narratives. All32have sentence-ended, non-truncated endings. No raw HTML, navigation/SEO blocks, code, system prompts or tool traces observed.
- **Material defects:** reversing wool as a repair action; incorrect definition of intelligent; dizzy/uncovered-file/blanket word salad; unexplained dark-location printing; unsafe generic navigation advice that any direction guarantees home; abandoned nail subplot; ceiling-mail lexical/causal failure. These drive seven holds.
- **Borderline material:** weakly joined episodes, forced vocabulary, unsupported final morals, and local state inconsistencies such as calling freshly drawn paper empty. Ten review flags distinguish these from an automatic exclusion decision.
- **Fiction is not automatically an error:** talking animals, magic flowers and explicitly magical toys can support coherent narratives. Sad endings need not be unresolved. A deaf character speaking is not a contradiction. Conversely, explicit word definitions and generalized practical advice still warrant scrutiny when embedded in fiction.
- **Scope limitation:** formulaic openings, recurring character names and tidy morals dominate. This can teach simple prose and local narrative dependencies, but supplies neither adult-domain factual reliability nor assistant request fulfillment, schema use, actual tool execution or observed-result faithfulness. No external factual checking was performed.

These are individual judgments from a small subjective stratified sample, not population defect rates or a direct statistical comparison to the earlier25/32retain sample. Retain means acceptable for fictional narrative learning on inspection, not general factual correctness. Per-document reasons are in judgments.json; full texts remain in sample.jsonl.
