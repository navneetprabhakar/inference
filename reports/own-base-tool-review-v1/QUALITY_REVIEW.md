# Independent tool-candidate verification

**No additional candidate exclusions are proposed from the checks performed.** This is a bounded consistency/semantics result, not training admission or proof that a student can use tools. Root custody remains authoritative; no protected-case contents/reasons were accessed.

The full frozen pool of5,922 lessons/8,223 current-turn rows passed independent verification in55.64seconds at nice10 with one CPU thread. The verifier did not call the candidate adapter or regenerate lessons. It replayed stored proposals through the broker and separately recomputed ordinary arithmetic/unit results, comparing observations, source labels, legacy projections, stable adapted histories and target metadata. It exercised6,009 actual observations and592 bound synthetic observations,135 error answers,736 result recalls,527 original-argument/unit recalls,171 within-turn chains,473 explicitly worded cross-turn chains,74 input corrections and1,793 prohibited-call turns without a call.

Known answer forms were checked against actual observations, including counterfactual returns rather than ideal arithmetic. Errors retained their observed message and asserted no numeric result. Chained inputs used preceding observed outputs; correction calls retained the deduction and used the corrected starting value. Missing-input question text matched the intended missing field and preceded the eventual call. No-call expression answers were structurally checked; operand-to-language grounding was inspected in the deterministic family sample, not independently parsed from every natural-language utterance.

Current policy system text, full history preservation, host-generated call binding, complete timestamp-free tool record and per-turn input/target/call metadata matched the stored candidates. `last_turn_row` revalidated current framing/reply reservations and masking. That masking check shares the production helper; it does not independently prove the helper implementation. Runtime/source pins remained unchanged after verification. Actual versus synthetic provenance uses the record source and exact fixture binding, not merely `dispatched=True`.

## Family review and custody update

Before custody arrived, one fixed minimum-hash representative per authored family/split was frozen (163families). Every representative's first request/call binding was inspected, with full follow-up deterministic checks covering every lesson. After root custody arrived, the same selection rule was applied to eligible IDs only:160families remain (127train/33exposed-development), with four representative replacements read in full. The three unavailable families were not replenished or exposed through protected reasons. Preliminary artifacts are preserved; final selection is `custody-selection.json`.

Final root-custody-eligible counts are **5,334train lessons /7,342rows /225,426 assistant targets**, and **422exposed-development lessons /583rows /17,834targets**. Family overlap and nonnull first-call-signature overlap are zero. There are67 eligible same-request/call groups with multiple observed numerical outcomes. This supports an observation-sensitivity diagnostic; it does not make development sealed or concept-independent.

The inspected authored requests bind arithmetic order, conversion direction and numeric roles appropriately. Clarifications, permission transitions, error reporting, readback, corrections and chains are represented. Counterfactual readings are explicitly fixture-labeled, not ordinary truthful arithmetic. Pure permission-change examples and later no-call recall should be scored on model attempts as well as broker dispatch: a broker-blocked forbidden proposal still indicates model noncompliance.

## Limits and reproducibility

Templates and numeric variation can be memorized. Only one natural-language binding per authored family was manually inspected; full-pool automation verifies deterministic output/observation consistency, not arbitrary language understanding. Error recovery is an explicit user-requested retry after policy resets, not autonomous recovery. The curriculum covers only calculator/converter fixtures and short bounded sessions, not external services or broad conversational competence. No-call expression operands are not comprehensively independently parsed from spelled-out prose. Recall coverage counters use known lexical trigger forms; do not interpret them as universal semantic coverage.

An initial verifier development attempt incorrectly looked for the unit on the final calculator call of a conversion chain. It flagged one candidate even though the first conversion correctly supplies the final answer unit. The verifier was corrected and the entire pool rerun; the initial failed development receipt is preserved as `verifier-development-attempt1.json`. This was not a candidate defect.

Reproduce with `verify.py` for full pool consistency and `verify_custody.py` for current eligible family selection/counts. Receipts and raw family samples are in ignored `data/own-base-tool-review-v1/`; source candidates/helpers are unchanged. Any final training preparation must bind these receipts and root custody, retain exposed-development separation, and report actual generated behavior without host answer repair or fallback.
