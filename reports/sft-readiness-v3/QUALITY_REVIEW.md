No dangling unfinished assistant frame or explicit unresolved correction caused by prefix selection was observed in these48; the review cannot certify omitted-parent intent or all unreviewed prefixes.

Original teacher systems were preserved and used for judging. Their later combination with host permission policy must preserve the task constraints and be rechecked under exact framing, reply reservation and current-turn-only masks. Historical teacher self-identity/cutoff claims should not be transferred as claims about the own-base model. Role-conditioned examples are legitimate only while their requested role remains represented in the eventual input.
