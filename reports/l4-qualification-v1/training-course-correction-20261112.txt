Decision
Keep the immutable 537M own-base checkpoint at 6,000,476,160 tokens. Do not spend the remaining budget blindly reaching 10B. First establish that the current model can learn the conversation/tool protocol in an isolated SFT branch. Do not deploy 4K, grow parameters or change base numerics during this investigation.

4. Own-tokenizer audit:4096general sessions supply2628987targets;2048tool sessions supply102951targets, only3.7684percent of the training objective. Average token loss is dominated by general prose despite tools being one-third of sessions. Assistant-end tokens are0.452percent of targets. These fractions explain weak observability; they alone do not prove causality.
5. General assistant completion median297tokens;87.42percent exceed128tokens, the diagnostic per-action generation budget. Tool text median6tokens. A generic longer reply can time out; however exact short commands and tool requests are still genuinely wrong. Increasing generation budget alone will not fix instruction adherence.
6. Inspected encoding uses shifted labels, assistant text/tool delimiters/end supervision, ignored user/tool observations, globally normalized valid-target accumulation. No confirmed off-by-one/masking fault found. Tests are useful but do not replace real checkpoint token-level measurements.
7. SFT uses pretraining optimizer moments and conservative fresh LR multiplier0.8*sqrt(32768/524288)=0.2. Upstream nanochat also supports optimizer warm-start and LR reset, so warm-start is not inherently a bug. The extra batch scale and short155update horizon remain hypotheses to test, not proven causes.

9. 6B watcher correctly blocked no-improvement. Controller later exhausted review wait and stopped the VM. The shutdown was a lifecycle-policy consequence, not evidence of a bad checkpoint. Later status reports were not remediation.

Ranked hypotheses
HIGH: diagnostic too small, prose-token-dominated and poor match to short protocol/termination tasks; full SFT not yet exercised.
MEDIUM: insufficient new-protocol/stop-token adaptation under short conservative schedule; optimizer carryover may affect adaptation.
MEDIUM: packing cross-conversation attention/position distribution or training/inference numerical difference. Needs direct comparison, not assumptions.
LOWER / NOT ESTABLISHED: irrecoverably bad base, model too small, need billions more teacher tokens. Cannot conclude these before learnability checks.

F. These are proposed engineering gates, not established model scores. Inspect complete multi-turn transcripts and repetition. Then evaluate untouched final acceptance. Only after clear gain consider finishing base10B or4Kadaptation; do not treat10B as more valuable than final usability under fixed budget.
