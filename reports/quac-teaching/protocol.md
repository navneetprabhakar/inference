# QuAC training admission protocol

1. Only the published train download is eligible. Split by canonical article family before choosing examples. Keep the publisher's validation/test files closed. A separate custodian screens public train histories against protected project materials.
2. Exclude sensitive visible passages, suspicious attribution notices, low-quality extraction artifacts, previously used article families and ambiguous question forms. Present deterministic, complete-sentence source windows chosen independently of target offsets.
3. For a multi-turn history, preserve adjacent original human questions with no intervening questions omitted. If the first selected question is mid-dialogue, require a standalone named anchor. Retain exact original response offsets and dialogue-act annotations in private provenance. Answerability is evaluated relative to the passage. A yes/no label is applicable only to a syntactically yes/no question. An original unanswerable sentinel is never a literal source answer and never appears in model-visible context.
4. On the final filter version, draw 12 hash-selected complete training histories per retained cohort using a fresh salt. A material failure is any user turn whose answer misidentifies the requested fact, asserts an unsupported polarity, falsely refuses an answer, or materially changes source meaning. An exposed sensitive passage also fails the cohort. Require at least 11/12 fully usable and no sensitive cases. Hold the offending history. Do not promote earlier failed cohorts wholesale. Report compounded weakness in multi-turn cases and maintain explicit non-human-review labeling.
