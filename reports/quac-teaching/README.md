# QuAC train-only intake

**83 histories, 97 QA turns, 78 distinct articles, 2,795 supervised tokens.** Twelve histories have adjacent original human follow-ups. Four include an unsupported question. Seventy-eight histories fit a 256-token model window. The release has limited utility for broad conversational repair: most of the surviving independent examples concern music releases, recordings and bands. The two-article validation and three-article test partitions are too small for performance claims.

The publisher identifies the QuAC data license as CC BY-SA 4.0. The older AllenAI loader references the original train-v0.2 archive while its card labels the license MIT. Preserve the publisher's attribution/share-alike terms when redistributing the extract, including source article links, titles, original offsets and transformation descriptions. The local train records preserve all these fields. The conversion is pinned to commit 768addbf2099da8efabd67d3a669f6483b813c1b. The 31,324,530-byte train parquet matched the advertised LFS SHA-256 067c94373d1a622339a73f16032d8060bb5dd989cd8ce2b3647363a8bc1d5b5f. Only this body was downloaded. [Publisher](https://quac.ai/) · [Original loader](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/quac/blob/2c1b73f07a629a338127b6a9761c64bf3e969179/quac.py) · [Pinned parquet](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/quac/blob/768addbf2099da8efabd67d3a669f6483b813c1b/plain_text/train/0000.parquet) · [QuAC authors and collection method](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1241/)

The raw train contains 11,567 dialogues. Article-first splitting and deterministic passage windows produced 1,216 candidate train histories, including 187 with consecutive turns. The final full-cohort sample failed admission. Extractive answer spans described a ski race when asked about studies and a Sony tournament when asked what happened. Yes/no examples included a purported good time for Peirce supported only by a name change and a clipped boxing result. Unsupported-answer examples included a passage discussing an award after questions about soundtrack success. As a result none of the general cohorts entered wholesale. Grounded word overlap alone still chose incorrect entities and relations. All twelve examples with followups in the released train file were individually reviewed against the presented passage.

The fallback cohort requires both the question and its answer to explicitly express one of a fixed set of easily checked relations. Every content stem in the question must appear in the answer, which must end as a complete sentence and contain at most 35 words. Across twelve hash-sampled histories, eleven were usable. The chart answer clipped at “no.” was held. Review salt was quac-relation-final-1, sorted by SHA-256 of the salt, original dialogue ID and decimal window start. The seed and unconditional earlier rejection decisions are preserved in admission.json. Rows outside the individual allowlist received sampled admission. Training examples are a derived dataset: first user turns add title/section/passage, targets use a validated original span, literal completion of a clipped span, literal year or a fixed unsupported answer as recorded in quac_teaching.py. Teacher followup metadata never substitutes for real student messages.

Reproduce from repository root with the project virtual environment:

    .venv/bin/python reports/quac-teaching/acquire.py
    .venv/bin/python quac_teaching.py
    .venv/bin/python reports/quac-teaching/freeze.py

freeze.py checks raw offsets, contiguous source turn IDs, actual visible passages, split disjointness, codec lengths, passage near-duplicates and article overlap with the earlier public SQuAD train pool. quac_teaching.examples(seed) loads only the hash-pinned frozen train split. Outputs are recorded in receipt.json. Protected project screening is delegated to the separate evaluation custodian. Retain this pool as supplemental grounding material only.
