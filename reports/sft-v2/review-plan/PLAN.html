SFT comparison and improvement plan

Training blocked by user instruction. Both 100M-base arms have trained 5M slots each. LR1 final evaluation is complete; LR3 final evaluation is still running. Neither 200M-base arm has started. This is a dated snapshot, not a live dashboard.

Recommendation

Provisionally retain 100M + 5M SFT at LR 3e-5 as the next research candidate, and LR 1e-5 as rollback. On the same 195-case development slice with greedy decoding and penalty 1.2, full single-tool success is 15/50 versus 5/50, grounded exact is 24/30 versus 22/30, and chat repetition is 11.7% versus 16.6%. LR3 is slightly worse on clarification (12/25 versus 13/25), and both have 0/30 complete chains. Development results are selection evidence, not the final held-out verdict. Neither model is ready as a reliable general assistant.

What the failures show

In the completed LR1 final test, 59/143 single calls are schema-valid, 25/143 use the exact arguments, and only 5/143 finish with the correct canonical answer. Several correct catalog calls return invented prices/stock despite correct tool results. In 63/100 clarification cases the output is “What is the tracking reference?” This indicates sensitivity to the test prefix, not just inability to produce English. Full tool chains fail despite many syntactically valid calls. More JSON-only supervision will not fix operand copying and result grounding.

Metric-by-metric plan

Targets are proposed engineering gates, not promised outcomes. Existing inspected tests are now known regression tests: never insert their exact examples into training and call later gains unseen generalization. Fresh evaluation families are essential. Some metrics are already at their ceiling; preserve them rather than claim every number must increase.

Sequence and ETA

1. Finish evidence and freeze selection

1–3 hours; current evaluation may take another 15–30 active minutes — Let the current LR3 final evaluation finish; keep both 200M arms blocked. Blind-review fixed samples from both 100M arms and preserve canonical raw metrics. LR3 at 5M is provisional candidate; keep LR1 as rollback. No four-way winner claim.

2. Fix dataset and evaluator weaknesses

6–10 engineering hours — Create training-only remediation examples and development suite; independently author 800–1,000 fresh final cases stratified by tools, operations, lengths, novel entities/aliases and trajectories. Split underlying scenario families, remove prefix cues, and keep the existing 756-case set as a known regression set. Add per-argument, conditional-result and chain-step diagnostics. Add blinded chat rubric; no paid judge.

3. Evaluate inference-only options

2–4 engineering/evaluation hours — Compare common decoder to schema-constrained calls and deterministic tool-result rendering using actual returned values. Keep raw model and assisted-system scores separate. Grammar fixes JSON shape, not wrong copied operands. Tune on dev only; do not repeatedly search the fresh test set.

4. Proposed bounded repair experiment — NOT AUTHORIZED OR STARTED

About 45–75 minutes training plus 3–6 hours evaluation — After review, two independent 2M processed-input-slot continuations from the selected SFT checkpoint, at LR 1e-5 and 3e-5. Same revised data/order, fresh optimizer, 50% tools/25% chat/10% grounded/15% English replay by nonpadding input tokens. Track actual assistant targets and full-LM replay targets separately. Compare 1M/2M dev milestones; choose by semantic task success and retention gates. Maximum 4M additional slots at this stage.

5. Decision gate

1–2 hours review; initial iteration approximately 2–3 working days total — Unlock fresh test once for selected candidate. Report paired case changes and uncertainty by scenario family, not inflated independent-template counts. Require meaningful tool and chat gains with grounding/abstention/error non-regression and retention within stated gates. If numeric copying and chains remain weak, stop scaling this recipe and reconsider pretraining/tokenizer capacity. Completing the two unstarted 200M arms would require separate approval; it is not silently included.

Research informing the plan

  • Hugging Face grammar guidance: constrains output structure; it does not establish correct argument values. Native MLX integration requires implementation work; this is not a claim that TGI runs our custom model unchanged.
  • Hammer: function masking and irrelevant-function training motivate renamed-schema and tool-selection robustness. Its results are not a guarantee for our 97M model.
  • InsCL: replay-based continual learning informs the retention ablation. The suggested 15% replay ratio is our experiment proposal, not a paper-derived optimum.

All proposed work stays laptop-local with public/local data and no cloud or paid teacher API. A broad English assistant may require substantially more pretraining; this bounded plan tests fixable data and execution weaknesses before committing to that expense.
