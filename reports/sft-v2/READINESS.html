Data corrections

Tool arguments are sampled independently of tool identity. Cities and natural note topics are disjoint between splits. All four arithmetic operations, six conversion directions and three chain routes are present after filtering. Numeric inputs include negatives and decimals; numeric ranges can overlap. Synthetic fixtures remain artificial, and limited clarification templates remain a weakness.

Scoring corrections

  • Exact call sequence, executable schema and canonical final-answer accuracy are reported separately.
  • Canonical answer matching allows only case/whitespace normalization. Wrong signs, extra numbers, negation and appended claims fail. Valid paraphrases may also fail: this is a conservative format-sensitive score, not semantic equivalence.
  • No-tool abstention no longer counts as answer correctness. Chat/no-tool quality remains unrated until blinded human review.
  • Primary comparisons use the same greedy decoder, penalty 1.2, seed 100. Expanded development sweeps are exploratory only.
  • Blind review exporter uses fixed first 30 chat and 30 no-tool cases per checkpoint with hidden model labels and an explicit rubric.
  • Fixed v1 evaluation crash caused by shadowing the retention-scoring function. v1 baseline generations exist but v1 final evaluation did not complete.

Validation and limits

18 tests pass, including loss equivalence for trimmed/padded inputs and adversarial scoring cases. Frozen data mask roundtrips, split separation, executable targets and exact budget simulation pass. Gold/negative scoring audit passes. One isolated real-model gradient update with checkpoint roundtrip passed; original checkpoint unchanged. This is not a memorization test, full evaluation run, or evidence of improved model quality. No training ETA is claimed from this small smoke test.
