OWN MODEL: FEASIBILITY REASSESSMENT — 14 NOVEMBER 2026
Decision: HOLD. The current recipe is not an evidence-backed route to a dependable
local conversation/tool assistant, much less CBSE+2 maths/science. A narrower
from-scratch learning experiment remains plausible, but recovery is unproven.
This supersedes the recommendation to launch the existing 4K corrective pilots
once infrastructure qualification passes. It does NOT authorize a replacement
model, GPU launch, more pretraining, or a larger budget.

1. WHAT WE ACTUALLY HAVE
537M randomly initialized parameters; 10,000,269,312 base tokens; 2K native context.
The SFT194 run saw 349 unique sessions and 43,183 unique assistant targets, repeated
8 times. Only 80 Codex natural conversations were trained. Later 3K–4K expansions
were not in that run. Repeated targets are not new behavioral diversity.
Strict CUDA result: 5/180 turns, 0/60 complete sessions. Exposed protected tools:
18/18. Current new short development suite: 2/54 turns, 0/18 sessions on MPS.
These are severe task failures, not a nearly finished assistant needing polish.
Different suites/devices are not directly interchangeable percentages.

Validation CE fell from 3.1210 to 2.3124 at step50, then rose to 3.6175 at194.
Final-checkpoint selection and lack of enforced behavioral stops allowed a
regressing run to finish. The last tiny training loss was a converter-only tail,
not the loss of the complete corpus. Matched step50/194 checks both passed only
4/16 turns (calculator cases); neither passed the 12 natural/state cases.
Rollback alone has not demonstrated recovery.

2. NEW LOCAL EXPERIMENT: SEPARATE LANGUAGE FROM CHAT PROTOCOL
Ran the hash-verified 10B base, SFT50 and SFT194 with the same tokenizer, float32
MPS runtime and prompts. No training, no network serving, no release exposure.
Source: probe_foundation.py; outputs: base10b.json, sft50.json, sft194.json.
Manually reviewed all 12 raw completions and all 36 candidate comparisons.

Twelve contrastive cloze questions, correct answer vs one chosen distractor:
                          Base10B    SFT50    SFT194
Correct preferred         9/12       8/12     7/12
State/relation subset     3/4        3/4      2/4
Basic science subset      4/4        4/4      4/4
Arithmetic subset         2/4        1/4      1/4
Sum and mean token NLL give the same rankings. Candidate lengths differ; both
are saved. These are investigator-authored exposed diagnostics, NOT generation
accuracy, a general benchmark, statistical proof of regression or CBSE readiness.

Raw completions establish some language ability, with important limits:
- Base correctly continues Arun's notebook as blue and parcel location as Kochi,
  but repeats the parcel sentence and repeats the library explanation.
- SFT50 produces coherent bird/library prose, but invents Mumbai as the parcel's
  latest location despite the supplied Kochi update.
- SFT194 initially says Kochi, then invents Wednesday/Thursday movements; its
  notebook continuation later describes both notebooks as blue.
- All three prefer stale Jaipur over current Surat in a separate cloze case.
- SFT194 additionally prefers the old owner over the recipient of a transferred mug.
Raw continuation is expected to continue; these fixed 32-token completions cannot
measure chat stopping. Newly invented story details are not automatically a
protocol failure. However, contradictory state and weak arithmetic exist outside
the chat wrapper. Therefore fixing end tokens/formatting alone is insufficient.
The base is neither proven useless nor proven adequate. A claim that SFT destroyed
all language knowledge would also be unsupported.

3. CAUSAL FINDINGS: WHAT IS CONFIRMED VS HYPOTHESIS
Confirmed process/data failures:
- Sparse behavioral curriculum: 43,183 unique assistant targets is the entire
  original SFT signal. Policy data had zero tools-enabled/no-call examples.
- Generalization was not a successful gate before continued training/promotion.
  Loss/checkpoint completion/protected calculators substituted for broad behavior.
- Original late-context corpus was not actually consumed; generation != training.
- Pretraining data provenance/packing checks do not verify factual education.
  The six-row source spot check even found an incorrect claim about snake smell;
  this is a warning about source reliability, not an estimate of corpus error rate.

Plausible contributors, not isolated causes:
- Narrow repeated SFT examples encourage memorized patterns and poor transfer.
- Inherited base optimizer moments and SFT LR may affect adaptation; no controlled
  reset-vs-carryover experiment has isolated this.
- Base training/content may be inadequate for intended reasoning. No broad base
  capability benchmark establishes its ceiling or necessary additional tokens.

Not established as causes:
- No confirmed universal tokenizer, target-mask or train/serve-prefix corruption;
  prior objective/mask/replay checks passed. EOS targets are supervised.
- A 192-token budget is not the sole issue: sampled 512-token retries still looped.
- The newly discovered qualification cadence bug affects the proposed restart
  experiment, not the cause of the already completed SFT194 failure.

4. WHY THE PROPOSED CORRECTION STILL MISSES THE MAIN QUESTION
The frozen candidate has 272 train sessions, 175,346 unique targets. The new short
corrective core contributes 5,111 targets (2.915%). Reviewed long data is useful,
but session balance does not imply loss balance.
Actual first10-update packing contains 104,361 supervised targets:
  new short natural 399; new short code/state 276; new short tools 2,260;
  inherited natural_dialogue_4k 42,948; long_context_natural 32,127;
  long_context_tool 26,351.
Thus new short natural+state = 675 targets, 0.647% of pilot target mass; all new
short lessons = 2.812%. Long examples may also teach these skills: this is not a
claim that only 0.647% of all useful supervision exists. It demonstrates that the
new targeted repair signal is heavily diluted under token-normalized loss.

The pilot simultaneously changes data, order, context2K->4K, batch and LR.
Source formula: fresh LR *0.8*sqrt(batch/524288)*multiplier. Proposed32768/.125
means .025*fresh LR; prior8192/1 means .1*fresh LR. New initial LR is one-quarter
of the old initial LR. Parent moments remain. A failure after10updates would not
identify whether data, dose, optimization, context extension or foundation failed.
A successful 4K forward pass demonstrates execution, not long-context learning.
Full development CE also favors long answers; short natural/state targets total
221/50,013. Report macro family behavioral results separately from aggregate CE.

5. FEASIBILITY OF THE GOAL
A) Own-from-scratch short dialogue, explicit state updates, narrow safe tools:
   worth a bounded learning diagnosis; no credible success probability yet.
B) Dependable open-ended local assistant: not supported by current evidence.
C) Broad CBSE+2 maths/science from these weights and a $15 repair run: no defensible
   delivery commitment. Knowledge coverage, mathematical reasoning and instruction
   following are distinct capabilities; longer conversations do not supply them.
D) A local system using a pretrained instruct model, retrieval and verified tools:
   an alternative engineering path if practical utility becomes the main goal;
   it would NOT count as success training Own from scratch. No replacement made.

External calibration, not an apples-to-apples comparison:
SmolLM2-360M's official card reports 4T pretraining tokens (~400x our10B), and only
7.43 GSM8K five-shot for its instruct model. This does not mean Own needs4T or
should blindly scale tokens; it shows parameter count alone and a token milestone
cannot justify general-assistant or school-maths claims.
https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M
TinyStories demonstrates coherent small models in a deliberately restricted
language domain. Narrow scope can be learnable; that does not establish broad
reasoning or reliable tool use for our model.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759

6. REVISED EXPERIMENT ORDER — PROPOSAL, NOT ACTIVATED TRAINING
First resolve learnability at native2K. Preserve the frozen4K corpus/release;
create any new diagnostic packet as a separate version with full provenance.
No hidden lowering of existing release gates and no automatic pilot extensions.

Stage0: experiment integrity and positive controls, locally first.
- Align all validation observations in uninterrupted/restart qualification and
  exercise the real training-loop orchestration, not just individual guard tests.
- Bind STOP lease, cumulative spend, single writer, recovery disk and independently
  verified GCS restore. Runtime qualification is separate from quality acceptance.
- Add generation scoring controls (known good/bad frames), check state semantics
  independent of custom protocol. Optionally compare a pretrained small model
  using its native template; do not force it through Own's tokenizer/protocol.
- Lock scenario-group splits before new data, all raw-output scoring rules and
  family target budgets. Diagnostic cases here are now exposed, never release.

Stage1: tiny fit experiment at native2K, from the preserved base.
- A proposed 24-session reviewed diagnostic packet: eight short dialogue/stop,
  eight state correction, eight tool-policy sessions including call/no-call.
- Verify it can learn its OWN training examples, including complete turns and
  actual executable calls. Proposed go gate: >=23/24 complete training sessions,
  all tool-policy prohibitions respected, finite gradients and checkpoint reload.
- This is an intentionally overfit plumbing/optimization control, NOT acceptance.
- Predeclare a finite exposure/update cap in a new signed contract before launch;
  benchmark throughput before pricing it. Do not silently change the current10 cap.
- If it cannot fit, inspect gradients, masks, per-family loss, stop-token margins,
  optimizer state and LR. Do not respond by generating thousands more examples.

Stage2: transfer, only if Stage1 passes and budget/authorization remains.
- Use separate scenario groups, new entities/numbers, varied wording and actual
  multi-turn state changes. Balance effective target mass at short context.
- Keep parent/context/batch/order fixed. Compare one optimizer/LR choice at a time
  within a preregistered comparison; no uncontrolled menu of rescue retries.
- Proposed DEVELOPMENT go gate (not a replacement release contract): >=90% strict
  turns and >=80% complete sessions in EACH short natural/state/tool family,
  no forbidden tool action, no protected regression. Count blocked follow-ups.
- Passing training but failing transfer means learned examples, not learned skill.
  If two bounded pilots fail, hold and diagnose; no automatic extension.
- These thresholds are engineering decisions, not confidence guarantees. Count
  sessions and uncertainty, and retain raw failures rather than cherry-picked demos.

Stage3: only after short transfer succeeds, add long-context supervision in an
explicit curriculum; compare matched2K/4K tasks and protect short performance.
Then full existing61-session development assessment; preselect candidate; consume
frozen63-session release ONCE; manually review every output; matched localMPS check.
The release is source-disjoint but Codex-authored like much of training: it is not
an independent-human benchmark. Add external/human evaluation before broad claims.

Stage4: school subjects require their own scoped curriculum and unseen assessments:
licensed/permission-compatible authoritative sources; generated examples checked
against sources and executable maths; split by problem family before variants;
units, diagrams, derivations and uncertainty scored separately. Retrieval/tool
success must be labeled system success, not intrinsic model knowledge. Deferred.

7. BUDGET, STOPPING, RESPONSIBILITY
$15 remains a proposed diagnostic ceiling, NOT the minimum price of recovering
conversation or finishing the revised stages. No new justified end-to-end budget
exists before learning/throughput evidence. Do not extrapolate the prior extension
spend to the cost of all10B tokens. Existing storage still costs money while off.
The conditional approval did not activate; this further material assessment change
must be reported before any GPU spend. All3VMs freshly observed TERMINATED.
Local base/50/194/605 preserved. No serving default changed; HTTP remains off.

Previous planning gave too much weight to test counts, corpus length and completed
training, and insufficient weight to a discriminating learnability experiment.
Those controls prevent operational failures; they do not prove we are teaching
what is missing. The next purchase must buy evidence about learning and transfer,
not another completed checkpoint. A failed small pilot does not prove impossibility;
a successful tiny fit does not prove a useful assistant. Report both honestly.
