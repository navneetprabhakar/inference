# Independent tool candidate review

Decision: do not admit the v1 tool candidates. Preserve both databases and manifests unchanged. Prepare a prospectively frozen v2 with the same target ceilings; no model evaluation, private acceptance access, or adaptive survivor refill informed this decision.

Root read all 84 primary prompt forms, 24 session form entries, common follow-ups, the complete builder, protocol and plan. The arithmetic ordering, correction, retry, missing-slot and no-call instructions support the planned operations. The session array, however, contains one calculator and one converter form: its boolean selector always uses one entry for a given family. These are not two selected paraphrases. Session v2 needs two independently authored and actually selected forms for each kind.

The principal answer defect is numeric serialization. The builder formats observations with nine significant digits even where prompts request exact or copied observed values. Independent audit of every retained observation and numeric answer found:

| Source | Lessons | Numeric readback answers | Answers differing from observed numbers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Primary | 4,284 | 4,278 | 928 |
| Four-call | 565 | 1,695 | 1,111 |

All 6,241 ordinary successful results agree with independently implemented Fraction arithmetic/conversion factors within floating-point tolerance. All 784 synthetic observation bindings and 96 division-by-zero errors agree with their declared provenance. The failure is in answer rendering, not those calculations. `numeric-audit-v1.json` binds the inspected databases and manifests and retains examples. This audit imports no generator helpers and is descriptive evidence, not a complete candidate-verification receipt.

V2 must preserve the exact observed number (normalizing integer-valued floats is harmless). Any intentional rounding must instead be requested explicitly in the prompt. It must retain whole-history refusal if exact representations exceed context. Do not shorten an overflowing teacher history or reuse the v1 acceptance claims.

Development conversion coverage is also incomplete: primary covers length/mass and sessions only length. Increase the fixed prospective development pool to 64 instances per family, with all 16 directions represented before selection; retain existing 30k/10k development target ceilings and report actual retained coverage. This change precedes any student training or evaluation. No cap redistribution or post-selection refill is authorized.

The family labels for primary tools are wording/scenario families, not disjoint mathematical skills. Train/development share basic operation semantics; development remains exposed diagnostic material. Heavy normalized-template concentration limits the generalization claim. Unchanged behavioral evaluation and protected acceptance, not this targeted curriculum's training loss, remain the decision criteria.

Still required for the revised candidates: independent full prompt/state/dependency verification, serializer/mask/prefix reload, historical/private and exposed-development exclusion union, and fresh admission/preflight. This review grants none of those.
