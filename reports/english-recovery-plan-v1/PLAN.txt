OWN ENGLISH RECOVERY PLAN v1
14 November 2026 — planning only; no training, dataset admission or GPU authorization.
Scope: improve the existing Own537M lineage. No replacement pretrained model.

1. DECISION AND DEFINITION OF SUCCESS
First prove short English learnability, then transfer, then local efficiency.
Do not buy more pretraining or expand3K–4K conversations before those gates pass.
This plan is an experiment sequence, not a promise that these weights can reach
broad assistant quality. It can end in a documented HOLD without another expansion.

Target v1: natural, concise English across everyday3–6turn conversations; follow
scope and corrections, remember explicit facts, summarize supplied text, clarify
missing information, acknowledge uncertainty, and stop cleanly. Native2048context.
Simple tools remain regression checks; maths/CBSE, factual breadth, advanced coding,
voice and4Kcontext are outside this release. Intrinsic model behavior is measured
separately from broker correctness. No rewriting failed answers in a host rule.

Acceptance proposals, to freeze BEFORE candidate training:
- English development: >=90% strict turns and >=80% complete sessions IN EACH of
  six families. Show counts, family macro scores and session-level uncertainty.
- English sealed release: >=95% strict turns overall, >=85% complete sessions;
  each family >=90% turns and >=80% sessions. Invalid/incomplete frames <=1%.
- Zero forbidden executed calls, fabricated claims of tool execution, or critical
  host-policy violations. Existing protected tool suite18/18 plus fresh tool and
  no-call release cases; calculators cannot compensate for English failure.
- All failures, context overflows, timeouts and blocked followups count. Score
  semantic correctness, relevance, constraints/state, naturalness and completion.
  A turn passes only if all applicable dimensions pass. Grammar alone is not enough.
- These are proposed engineering thresholds, not observed scores or statistically
  established reliability. Do not weaken them after seeing a candidate's results.
- Local product targets, provisional until baseline: warm p95first-token<=2s at
  <=512input tokens; median decode>=15tokens/s for128output tokens; peak process
  memory<=12GiB on this24GiBMac; no sustained swap growth or crash over20sessions.
  Report cold-load and1536-token-prefill separately. Quality gates take priority;
  a fast failing model is not efficient. Freeze performance targets after baseline,
  before optimization, and disclose any change rather than moving the goalposts.

6. WORK PACKAGE D — SHORT ENGLISH CURRICULUM, ONLY AFTER FIT GO
Create a NEW version; never regenerate corrective-corpus-v1 or english-first-v1.
Start with at most300reviewed training sessions; ceiling600only if a measured
missing family needs it. These are workload ceilings, not required volumes.
Use3–6turn dialogues, mostly20–100token assistant replies, some appropriate one-line
answers and some150–250token grounded explanations. No forced length or filler.
Everything, including policy overhead and response reserve, must fit native2K.

Proposed assistant-target distribution across the production curriculum:
-25% everyday dialogue, natural acknowledgement, preferences and appropriate closing.
-20% conversational state: entity tracking, ownership vs possession, corrections.
-15% grounded reading: summarize/extract/answer using supplied English text.
-15% instruction following: tone, length, list counts, rewrite and scope constraints.
-10% clarification and uncertainty: missing referents, insufficient evidence.
- 5% conversation management: topic switch/return and explicit memory limits.
-10% narrow tool retention: actual calculate/convert trajectories and corrections.
These sum100%. Report unique tokens AND replay-weighted mass per family. Seeded
packing must expose every family early; do not let long inherited rows dominate.
Policy modes are an orthogonal axis: aim45%disabled/no-call,45%enabled/no-call,
10%enabled/call by session count; report token distribution separately. Freeze
realized balances before training. No positive calls in the isolated fit packet.

Data origins and use:
- Human-origin: selectively review English OpenAssistant trees. Keep whole trees
  in one split; select coherent, high-quality branches; remove duplicates/PII,
  unsupported claims and low-quality answers. Human origin does not guarantee quality.
- Human-origin optional: Dolly short grounded QA, rewriting and summarization.
  Preserve source context and provenance. Its CC-BY-SA license differs from
  OpenAssistant's Apache2.0; source obligations must be explicitly reviewed before
  admission/distribution. This plan is not a legal clearance.
- Codex: generate targeted everyday scenarios, state corrections, clarification,
  balanced tool/no-call examples, and natural paraphrases. Teacher/model/version,
  generation prompt hash, seed/scenarioID and reviewer decisions are mandatory.
- Optional external synthetic: selected smol-smoltalk examples for simple everyday
  style. This source is synthetic, NOT counted as organic/human coverage. Do not
  import all rows or assume its success transfers to Own.
Aim40%human-origin/60%synthetic assistant-target mass among English-only lessons.
If appropriate human data is unavailable, report the shortfall; do not pad or
mislabel synthetic data. Revisit the mix before freezing, never silently mid-run.

Full review/admission workflow:
1. Assign source tree/document/scenario/template GROUP to train/dev/sealed release
   before producing variants. No sibling paraphrases or entity swaps cross splits.
2. Generate/curate batches <=25sessions. Correctness, naturalness, state consistency,
   answer relevance, clean endings and source support must be reviewed on EVERYturn.
3. Keep explicit world-state ledgers for correction lessons; derive reference
   assertions from those ledgers. Replay EVERYtool observation through the broker.
4. Validate schema, tokenizer, masks, context and policy; exact and near-duplicate
   audit across all splits. Synthetic variants never increase unique scenario count.
5. Reject unresolved rows. Record fixes/versioning and rerun affected checks.
6. Freeze hashes and family token/mode coverage. Export an actual packed-update
   receipt proving which rows/targets appear before the finite update limit.
Codex generation plus Codex review is not independent human evaluation. A separate
review pass is useful but must not be advertised as independent authorship.
