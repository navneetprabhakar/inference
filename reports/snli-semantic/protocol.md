## Train-only semantic admission

Keep three-way original relation labels and vote lists. Exclude all Visual Genome prefix IDs, missing labels, disagreements among multiple annotators, sensitive descriptors, noisy text, ambiguous tentative constructions and identical premise/hypothesis strings. Group all public train examples by image ID plus transitively identical normalized premises before selecting validation or inspecting training samples. Remove conflicting copies of a normalized pair. Prefer recorded independent unanimous agreement when duplicate copies agree.

For each qualified annotation-count tier and each semantic label, read 12 hash-selected train pairs. Accept broad strata only when ≥11/12 relations are defensible under the same depicted event and all rows pass the sensitive-text gate. Unanimous annotators can still make context-dependent mistakes; material errors or substantial implicit assumptions can hold an entire stratum. A narrower stratum must have a separately stated rule and fresh-salt review. Fitting must keep single-author and independently judged relations separately countable/samplable. Pairs are for a three-way auxiliary classifier only. Entailment is directional and neither sufficient nor necessary for exact wording equivalence.
