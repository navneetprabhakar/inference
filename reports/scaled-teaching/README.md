# Scaled instruction pool

**1,052 training histories are available for a separate fallback experiment.** There are 813 one-exchange and 239 two-exchange histories. The training serializer counts 184,663 inputs and 63,594 assistant targets. All histories fit within 467 inputs. At a 256-token limit, 829 fit, so length filtering must be disclosed or the trainer must use a longer context.

| Admission policy | Histories | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Dolly closed QA | 266 | Paired passage, short question and answer. Answer numbers must occur in the passage, and at least 84% of nontrivial answer words must be present. Compound, comparative and obviously truncated cases are filtered. |
| Dolly classification | 260 | Both instruction and answer are from the published source; selected common categories include numbers, instruments, countries, materials and animals. |
| Everyday creative conversations | 209 | First two substantive exchanges from the explicit train parquet. Removing the canned greeting retains actual prompts and their paired source replies. |
| SmolTalk rewrite | 245 | Only the original `smollm-rewrite-30k` constituent. The source rewrite instruction becomes part of the user prompt, followed by the complete source draft. The original response is retained. Number additions, new named words, handles, and large unsupported lexical changes are screened. |
| Individually reviewed additions | 72 | Selected replies from the other cohorts after reading those specific histories. The parent cohorts failed sampled admission or were too small for it. |

**980 histories have sampled-cohort admission.** Each met row-level filters. The assistant read a fixed 12-history audit draw for each approved cohort. `review.json` records the sample IDs and exceptions, and `selected-ids.jsonl` labels the admission of each history. This process selects training data. Measured model benefit and independent protected-set clearance are separate gates.

**Scope.** The four-source total is 560 Dolly, 230 Everyday, 247 SmolTalk and 15 OASST histories. Exact normalized histories, duplicate concatenated user prompts and near-identical prompts sharing at least 80% of five-word shingles relative to the larger prompt are dropped. Existing broad Dolly row IDs and passages, prior OASST trees and prior Everyday selections are excluded. Template-like greetings, repeated topics and semantically related situations can remain. In particular, the rewrite slice repeats a handful of instructions over different supplied drafts, many of them emails. A thousand histories do not establish a thousand independent instruction formats.

## Quality findings and use

The initial sample contained a fabricated local concert schedule, risky household suggestions, bad physical-science explanations and source-grounding errors. Local and seasonal assertions, important action/identity claims, sensitive topics and contradictory or poorly formed answers prompted narrower filters. The entire Everyday science, leisure and practical cohorts, permissive Dolly extraction/summarization and open-ideas cohorts, and generic OASST cohorts were excluded from automatic admission after repeated errors. `protocol.md` and `review.json` distinguish historical rejection evidence from the approved cohorts. Human review, exhaustive factual verification and protected scenario-family overlap checks remain open.

Use `scaled_teaching.examples(seed)` to produce reproducibly shuffled role/text histories. Run `.venv/bin/python scaled_teaching.py` to re-read the pinned local train sources, reconstruct the selected histories, validate every history against the frozen IDs and serializer, and verify the seed-713 hash. Seed 713 reproduces `f1d28e8f0989ae6e95aa5f0aa41252e97afcf68e9743d2829f059559d635706f`. Rebuild checks use CPU only, and the source files are read-only. No trainer or serving process is invoked.
