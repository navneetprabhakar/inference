# Compositional teaching material

`compositional_teaching.examples(seed)` returns shuffled four-message histories for fresh training. `records(seed)` returns each history with its task family, wording form, pair ID, paired member and the underlying variable changed between members. Every record is original synthetic text. The generator has no dependency on evaluation files, model predictions or corpus archives.

At the working seed 1459, 76 manually authored forms yield 7,803 complete pairs and 15,606 unique two-turn conversations. All histories fit within 125 tokens under the dialogue-v1 serializer. In every pair the second required answer changes; in 665 pairs the first answer also changes. Competing names, identifiers, factual assignments and order remain internally consistent in both worlds. Fact order and the wording of queue and route descriptions vary by seeded draws. Names and literal identifiers are generated pseudowords; no external uniqueness or realism guarantee is inferred.

The nine skills are introduction and recall, contextual greetings, binding attributes to competing entities, corrections with a prior or superseded state, ordering, recognizing missing evidence, asking for clarification, constrained rewrites, and simple conditional reasoning. The seed changes world values and the shuffled order. It does not create new linguistic forms. If estimating generalization, reserve entire question forms or independent author-written cases rather than merely shuffling rows. Exact scores on training forms must not be treated as conversational release evidence.

`manifest.json` includes source, per-family counts, longest history, one case from each family, and the SHA-256 of UTF-8 `json.dumps(examples(1459), sort_keys=True, ensure_ascii=False)`. `span-coverage.json` verifies both members of every pair and estimates how much training support is available for the current literal-span reader: 20,264 assistant turns have a source span, while 10,948 require deferral to a generative response. All encoded prefixes fit the reader budget. The curriculum itself was created without inspection of protected evaluations. The independent overlap custodian controls any exclusions before fitting.
