Memory annotation review is separate from dialogue validity. Repeated facts, repeated constraints, partial composite targets, and topical cues must be distinguished. A topical mention of an atlas does not repeat its loan status; writing the names Aditi/Omar does not repeat the prohibition on renaming them. Conversely, a fresh paraphrase of a no-repair authorization or conditional lift offer is substantive support. Unsupported delay claims receive no delayed-memory credit while otherwise correct English remains eligible as a candidate.

The corpus is narrowly weighted: 135 correction and 135 memory conversations out of 310, with only seven marked ordinary. There are 28/59/75/90/44/14 conversations with 3/4/5/6/7/8 assistant turns. This is a focused curriculum component, not a balanced English release curriculum. Native2K encoding is a separate parent check; no 3K–4K length claim follows from these data.

Concrete leakage grouping should capture the latent state change, not just the hobby or nouns: latest-versus-old field values; temporary-versus-permanent transfer; owner/holder/recipient distinctions; proposed-versus-authorized actions; optional-versus-required participation; allowed audience/channel restrictions; and persistent hidden details in fictional/drafting tasks. The fresh review manifest includes mechanism and template hints for every group. A future development set needs preassigned source/scenario/template groups and independently generated mechanisms; these exposed train candidates must not be recycled into pristine release claims.
