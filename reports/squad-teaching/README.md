# Human-authored passage QA, SQuAD 1.1

**702 training histories contain 1,676 human questions grounded in 82 Wikipedia articles.** Each history presents one original paragraph once, followed by one to three original questions. There are 582 histories with multiple questions about the supplied paragraph. Most follow-up questions are independently answerable from the paragraph. This is evidence for reading comprehension; the examples alone do not teach broad conversation, user corrections, or tool routing.

## Source and custody

- SQuAD 1.1 by Pranav Rajpurkar, Jian Zhang, Konstantin Lopyrev and Percy Liang supplies crowdworker questions about Wikipedia passages. The pinned raw publisher training file has 87,599 questions over 442 articles and SHA-256 `3527663986b8295af4f7fcdff1ba1ff3f72d07d61a20f487cb238a6ef92fd955`. The dataset is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Article links, raw paragraphs, and modification notes are retained locally. The archived repository's software license covers its code separately. [Publisher dataset card and license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkar/squad) · [Pinned training file](https://github.com/rajpurkar/SQuAD-explorer/blob/eee5fdbf62f8613a7812b03419e6b29617b74fd1/dataset/train-v1.1.json) · [Original paper](https://aclanthology.org/D16-1264/) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- The earlier archive had 204 article components assigned to training after reserving 100 development and 100 other protected components. It also excluded cross-source and near-duplicate article families. This build uses only those 204 allowed components, rechecks their deduplicated source paragraphs against the publisher train JSON and excludes all previously replayed/individually reviewed paragraph representatives. It never reads the official SQuAD dev JSON. Archived approval of historical runs was not adopted as a review of these new examples.
- Current-branch pretraining Wikipedia article titles, all available QuAC train article titles, and SQuAD article groups sharing a normalized eight-word sequence with existing broad/scaled language teaching text were excluded. The internal split is assigned by original archive component hash, keeping linked articles together. The resulting training and internal validation share zero article groups, titles or paragraph hashes. Some source articles have other paragraphs that historical models trained on; their weights were never imported into this project.
- Every retained question reuses a human question and the first supplied human answer verbatim. Offsets are Python Unicode character indexes in the untouched original paragraph. The derived history introduces the passage and groups questions sharing it. It does not generate a synthetic question, paraphrase an answer or crop a context using the answer. Frozen raw outputs remain under `data/squad-training-v1/{train,validation}.jsonl`; only metadata and sanitized review results belong in Git.

## Review and limitations

An initial topic screen missed religious/ancestry material; those article topics and sensitive terms were removed before admission. A first stratified quality review of 32 further candidates identified omitted qualifiers, partial answers, and a sports question that attributed a basketball team to football. A second independent selection of 32 different training questions checked all eight WH categories after tightening location and answer-boundary rules. Judgment was by the assistant against the full original paragraph, without independent human review. The second review held five paragraphs, including a question about an electron's frequency rather than the incident light frequency, and an installation attributed to the wrong organization. The first review held seven. The accepted population was structurally rechecked; most questions received no individual semantic adjudication.

What-questions account for 1,040 of 1,676 training turns; only 12 start with why. Historical assertions can be stale, and an exact span may omit part of a correct answer or answer a misleading question. Formulations and sentence structures overlap across articles. These counts do not imply 1,676 independent conversations or proven model capability. The article and reuse constraints intentionally kept the number of distinct fresh paragraphs below 1,000.

## Reproduction

Using the existing archive and tokenizer assets, run `.venv/bin/python squad_teaching.py build` to create a fresh `data/squad-training-v1` directory and `.venv/bin/python squad_teaching.py freeze` to apply the recorded review decisions. The train API validates the published training-file hash and split membership before use. `verification.json` independently checks the real training serializer and every answer offset in both derived splits. `protocol.md` and `review.json` retain the preregistered review rule, original sample IDs and decisions. An external custodian handles protected-set overlap independently.
