# Independent assessment — 18 November 2026

Scope: independent agent with no previous conversation, reading repository instructions, then code, saved measurements and raw outputs. No new inference, training, cloud actions or frozen-data edits. This is an evidence audit, not an independent release evaluation. Historical recommendations were treated as hypotheses.

## Verdict

Ganesh-base is not yet a usable context-aware conversational model. It has learned English continuations and can memorize short conversational examples. The evidence does not establish that its foundation is incapable of the goal, that another 300–500 sessions will suffice, or that optimizer reset is the right intervention. The next expenditure should buy a discriminating learning experiment, not another tiny-example fit or a broad corpus campaign.

The highest-confidence failure is a joint problem: inadequate transfer from narrowly supervised examples, and a severely suppressed native termination token. These coexist; solving termination alone does not establish contextual reasoning.

## Evidence and ranked causes

1. **Narrow SFT coverage and memorization: strong evidence.** The latest training result records 480 consumed conversations but only 24 distinct training sessions, 13,120 replayed assistant positions versus 656 distinct positions, and 30 updates. At step30, fit content CE is 0.00395 while development content CE is 3.93990. Development content worsened from 2.63940 at step20 while termination improved. The saved review reports 21/24 passing fit sessions and zero complete passing development sessions. I inspected the raw full development outputs and sampled fit outputs: exact training interactions are reproduced fluently, while new interactions repeat requests, ignore corrections or lose state. These are substantive failures, not merely strict formatting. Evidence: `training/result.json`, `content-end-reassessment.json`, `raw/development-00000030.json`, `raw/exposed_fit-00000030.json` relative to this assessment directory (the training result is `assessment/training/result.json` from the campaign root).

2. **Native framing/termination adaptation: strong evidence, incomplete causal attribution.** `nano_chat_format.py` explicitly supervises assistant-end; `nano_sft.py` shifts that mask correctly. The pinned model softcaps logits with `15*tanh(logit/15)`. Saved endpoint measurements show extremely suppressed ends in the base and delayed recovery. This explains many invalid-frame scores, but not why a completed reply repeats “Please tell me what the ending of the story was like” after being asked not to ask, or why first-message preference recall returns the latest request. Teacher-forced endpoint top1 is not free-running completion probability. Raw neighbor-chat output starts with relevant prose and degrades into subscription boilerplate; counting it as failed is appropriate, but 0% valid conversations must not be paraphrased as zero English knowledge.

3. **Weak foundation contextual competence: plausible, not demonstrated as the limiting cause.** The saved base completion correctly binds Arun to his blue notebook and gives Kochi as the parcel's latest location; other contrastive questions fail. The newer six-pair panel yields weak results, but supplied-choice likelihood is not generation, mean-token scoring changes sequence comparisons, and six families cannot establish a capability ceiling. The actual model has approximately 537M parameters; “base10b” denotes training exposure, not a 10B-parameter model. The checkpoint metadata identifies 19,074 base updates. I did not reconstruct the full consumed source stream, language distribution, duplication or contextual-example quality; exposure sufficiency remains unverified. Evidence: `reports/feasibility-deep-dive-v1/base10b.json`, `deep-review/probe_counterfactuals.py`, `runs/ownbase-budget50-v1/base-9b-result.json`.

4. **Optimizer carryover: real code behavior, low causal support.** `nano_sft.py:486–489` creates an optimizer, loads parent state, then resets learning rates. Saved EOS moments at base are tiny, contradicting a specific claim that stale EOS second moments prevent adaptation. Shared-layer momentum and step-counter effects remain possible. No matched reset/retain experiment establishes benefit; do not make this the first remedy solely because it is easy to change.

## What the existing interpretation cannot establish

- Fit versus development is not a clean IID generalization experiment: many fit examples are constrained rewrites; development includes broader social and reasoning interactions. The gap measures both distribution shift and memorization. It cannot select a required session count.
- The aggregate CE guard did catch deterioration in unseen reference content. Disabling it because generated termination improved would discard useful evidence. Conversely, CE-selected step20 is demonstrably not the best usable chat model; generation and content/end metrics must be separate selection signals.
- Packing diagnosis matching endpoint counts does not prove packing is harmless. `BestFitConversations.next_row` concatenates conversations without a per-document attention reset. This remains a train/serve context confound, although the small packed-versus-isolated CE differences do not support it as the main observed EOS cause.
- Earlier 349-session SFT supplied only 80 natural-dialogue sessions; 269 tool/correction sessions do not represent broad English coverage. A 31-session seed curriculum is not a comprehensive corrective dataset.
- A failed 23/24 fit gate is a valid contractual stop. It is not a scientific requirement that every future learning design must first memorize those same 24 examples. Any replacement must be prospective and diagnostic; old failure remains failed.

## Concrete next experiment

First prepare a local, no-training diagnostic panel: 48 counterfactual pairs (a bounded diagnostic design choice, not a sufficiency guarantee) across eight explicit capabilities (entity binding, corrections, temporal state, negation, constraints, uncertainty, paraphrase, delayed recall), with six independently authored scenarios per capability. Use fresh scenarios, balanced answer swaps, and both plain completion and production-native chat. Test base10b, fit30 and SFT194 at fixed decoding budgets. Score generated semantic answers separately from legal termination, then full raw rollouts; teacher-forced likelihood is secondary. Report pair and family counts, not inflated turn counts. Keep this exposed diagnostic separate from release.

**Decision rule:** If plain generated answers substantially outperform native chat within the same scenarios, prioritize a framing-adaptation pilot. If both fail the same capabilities, prioritize targeted content learning. If outcomes are mixed or parent ranking unstable by family, hold cloud work and improve the measurement rather than choose a winner from an aggregate.

**Conditional learning pilot:** On the chosen own-weight parent, use a new reviewed, family-balanced corpus with scenario/template-disjoint development; compare one narrowly specified intervention with an unchanged control under equal assistant-target exposure and identical checkpoint evaluation. For framing-dominant failure, a temporary special-token-only warm-up followed by the identical SFT schedule is a testable hypothesis, not an approved fix. For content-dominant failure, compare coverage-diverse contextual examples against an exposure-matched narrow replay control. Do not combine optimizer reset, EOS reweighting, new data and new LR in one arm. Preserve existing regression stops; new prospective design must include content CE, endpoint behavior and generated session performance. Stop an arm on content regression or numerical failure; stop the experiment if improvements stay confined to training examples or one template family. Advance only on replicated held-out family improvements without lost core capabilities; this is learning evidence, never release acceptance.

Exact update counts, success thresholds and GPU budget cannot honestly be finalized before panel results, data target counts and runtime measurements exist. Session-count and cost promises would be guesses. Prerequisites remain a fresh versioned contract, reviewed data/splits, fixed finite budget/reserve, relevant restart/backup readiness and the unresolved scheduled provider-STOP fault evidence. No automatic continuation of the closed campaign.
