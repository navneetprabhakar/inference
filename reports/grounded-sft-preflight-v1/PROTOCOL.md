Useful long-context behavior remains a separate public-development diagnostic; no sealed cases are opened.

The public CLI is a foreground watchdog, not a background agent. Its effective hard deadline is min(20-minute active,30-minute calendar)=20minutes and starts before child creation/verification; it owns a new child process group, sends termination with10seconds remaining and escalates with2seconds remaining, leaving cleanup inside the20-minute deadline. The30-minute calendar limit is an outer bound and is currently redundant; it never extends active work. An inherited one-use pipe ticket guards the internal child entrypoint. No retry occurs. Child success remains PASS_CHILD_PENDING_WATCHDOG; only a successful on-time foreground controller publishes hardware-receipt.json as PASS, bound to watchdog.json. Timeout/interruption preserves partial ledger and never publishes a passing receipt. Only the owned child/process group is targeted.

The inherited worker ticket, child proof, signed ledger time limits and final parent validation all bind the same20-minute effective hard bound. A native MPS hang cannot consume the otherwise unused30-minute calendar allowance. Late child exit or late proof validation cannot publish a final passing receipt.
