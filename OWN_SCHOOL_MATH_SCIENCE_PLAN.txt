OWN MODEL — MATHEMATICS AND SCIENCE THROUGH CBSE CLASS XII
Prepared: 13 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata
Status: detailed follow-on proposal. Finish the current authorized run first.

1. DECISION, SCOPE AND START CONDITIONS

Objective: teach our own 536,871,738-parameter model school-level mathematics,
Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with independently measured knowledge, reasoning,
tutoring and tool use. Preserve own-base lineage; no pretrained replacement.
English first. Hindi/bilingual instruction is a later separately evaluated phase.
Text and structured tables first. Image-based geometry, circuits, graphs and lab
apparatus are NOT covered by a text-only model; textual descriptions are a separate
assessment track, not a substitute for claiming visual competence.

Class XII is substantial: calculus, probabilistic reasoning, electromagnetism,
chemical equilibrium and biological mechanisms. This is a staged research target,
not a capability promised by model size, training loss or data volume.

Before executing this proposal:
- Complete current bounded base continuation and frozen two-arm SFT diagnostics.
- Review generated conversations, protected tools and historical retention; preserve
  the best checkpoint even if both new candidates fail.
- Verify GCS backups, stop the current VM, reconcile the cost estimate and record
  local checkpoint identities and actual MPS usability.
- Local HTTP deployment remains stopped at the user's request. Do not restart it
  automatically for this proposal; command-line evaluation can be used later.
- Freeze this proposal's parent checkpoint only after current results are known.
- No school corpus enters the current frozen training packet. No new VM, training,
  paid API fallback or spend is authorized by this planning document.

2. CURRICULUM AND COVERAGE MAP

Use the official 2026–27 CBSE syllabus as the exam-scope authority for IX–XII.
Use current NCERT materials to map prerequisite coverage below that level. Verify
class/edition changes before ingestion; older chapter lists can be stale.
Build concept IDs, prerequisite edges and explicit exclusions, not a book dump.
The categories below are a proposed dependency sequence, not a certified syllabus.

A. Foundations, roughly III–VIII
Math: place value, signs, four operations, fractions, decimals, ratios, percentages,
units, estimation, simple equations, elementary shapes, area/volume and data.
Science: measurement, observations versus explanations, matter, heat/light/sound,
simple forces/circuits, living systems and environments. Basic scientific method.

B. Secondary, IX–X
Math: number systems, polynomials, linear/quadratic equations, coordinate geometry,
proofs, similarity, introductory trigonometry, mensuration, statistics/probability.
Science: motion/forces/energy, electricity/light; reactions, acids/bases, metals and
carbon; cells, life processes, reproduction/heredity and ecological relationships.

C. Senior secondary, XI–XII
Math: functions, trigonometry, sequences, combinatorics, complex numbers, coordinate
geometry, limits/derivatives/integrals, differential equations, matrices/determinants,
vectors/3D geometry and probability, limited to the mapped current syllabus.
Physics: units/uncertainty, mechanics, thermal physics, oscillations/waves,
electrostatics/current/magnetism, induction/AC, optics and modern physics.
Chemistry: mole calculations, structure/bonding, thermodynamics/equilibrium,
solutions/electrochemistry/kinetics, inorganic and organic chemistry.
Biology: diversity, structure/function, physiology, reproduction, genetics/evolution,
biotechnology and ecology. Include causal explanations, not only vocabulary.

For every concept record: grade, official scope reference, prerequisites, definition,
assumptions, units, representations, common misconceptions, worked-problem families,
verification method, source eligibility, train/dev/test family IDs and coverage.

3. ORGANIC SOURCES — HUMAN-AUTHORED MATERIAL

Organic means human-authored explanations, exercises, marking schemes and reference
material. Existing model-generated datasets are not organic. Source discovery is
not ingestion approval. Record exact edition/revision, not just a website name.

Priority 1: CBSE syllabus, sample papers and marking schemes
Role: curriculum map, exam formats and evaluation rubrics. Preserve official sample
paper families for external checks where eligible; do not train on their solutions
and then present their score as unseen performance. Public-paper contamination from
prior base training may be unknown, so fresh private cases remain primary.
Source: https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html
Sample papers: discover the applicable year from the same official portal.
Status: reference/evaluation candidate; training reuse assessed per artifact.

Priority 2: NCERT textbooks and exemplars
Role: Indian terminology, conceptual progression, level and scope cross-checks.
https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php
NCERT states these textbooks are copyrighted and restricts republication. Do not
assume downloadable PDFs provide blanket model-training or public-corpus rights.
Default: reference-only; direct ingestion needs a documented compatible permission
basis for the exact use. Do not use Codex paraphrasing to bypass source restrictions.
Raw PDFs/extracted chapters must not enter this public Git repository by default.

Priority 3: Siyavula explicitly CC-BY, unbranded editions
Role: core organic school maths and physical/natural science explanations/exercises.
https://siyavula.github.io/work-oer.html
The catalog distinguishes CC-BY editions from branded CC-BY-ND editions. Select the
actual permissively licensed download and verify its embedded license and version.
Do not generalize that license to interactive exercises or every hosted PDF.
Map South African curriculum content to CBSE; omit out-of-scope sections and retain
attribution. This is a promising first acquisition source, not yet an ingested set.

Priority 4: OpenStax
Candidates: Prealgebra, Elementary/Intermediate Algebra, Algebra and Trigonometry,
Calculus, College Physics, Chemistry and Biology, using only mapped school sections.
https://openstax.org/subjects
https://help.openstax.org/s/article/Licensing-information-of-OpenStax-textbooks
Current official licensing guidance says CC BY-NC-SA. Do not rely on old blanket
CC-BY descriptions. Pin each edition/license; keep conditional/noncommercial assets
separate from any unrestricted release. Record permitted processing, attribution
and redistribution obligations before ingestion; model-release implications are
not resolved merely by calling the project personal.

Optional: Wikibooks/Wikiversity, selected pages only
Role: gap filling and alternative explanations; variable quality requires stronger
review. Per-page revision, license, imported-material exceptions and attribution

https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use

Excluded by default: Khan Academy scraping/training
Its current published terms guidance explicitly disallows AI/ML training, testing
or development using its content/data. Do not ingest it or feed its content to a

https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/42929097425037-What-s-allowed-and-not-allowed-under-Khan-Academy-s-Terms-of-Service
Also exclude scraped coaching sites, answer marketplaces and unknown-license PDFs.

Acquisition manifest fields:
source_id, URL, title, publisher, authors, edition, accessed_at, file_sha256,
license_text/hash, attribution, permitted_use, redistribution_status, scope_tags,
page/section anchors, extraction_version, reviewer and eligibility decision.
Keep reference-only, conditional and training-eligible pools physically separate.
This is an operational selection policy, not a blanket legal determination.

4. GENERATED SOURCES — CODEX AND PROGRAMMATIC GENERATORS

A. Codex-authored original lessons and problems
Use the existing Codex CLI generation route, currently recorded in the handoff as
gpt-6.1-terra, with the single-generator lock. Record actual returned model/version
and settings; verify availability when execution begins. No silent model switch,
quota reset or paid API fallback. Subscription access is not unlimited capacity.

Input: concept ID, prerequisite assumptions, level, allowed source anchors if any,
problem-family specification, permitted tools and required output schema.
Output: original problem, public worked solution, concise answer, units/domain,
misconception tags, proposed checks and source lineage. Request useful pedagogical
steps, not private hidden reasoning or long theatrical self-talk.

Do not show held-out cases, private seeds or reference answers to the teacher.
Separate generation from review. A second Codex review pass is useful but correlated
with the first; teacher agreement is not independent mathematical verification.
Reviewers must attempt counterexamples, not only confirm fluent explanations.

B. Programmatic problem generation
Use audited local generators with exact rational/integer or symbolic ground truth.
Examples: fraction arithmetic, linear/quadratic equations with domain constraints,
unit conversion, kinematics under stated assumptions, stoichiometric balances and
small exact-probability spaces. Codex can write generator code and naturalize wording.
Independently test generator and oracle; do not validate both with the same formula
copied twice. Preserve canonical problem and parameters to catch semantic drift.

Candidate external generator: Google DeepMind mathematics_dataset, a school-level
question/answer generator. Inspect pinned code/data licenses separately and verify
reproducibility before reuse. Treat as programmatic synthetic data, not organic.
https://github.com/google-deepmind/mathematics_dataset

C. Multi-turn tutoring and tool workflows
Generate: explanation -> student misconception -> correction -> changed condition;
choose equation -> tool call -> observed result -> units -> later result recall;
insufficient information -> clarification; tool unavailable -> honest limitation.
Actual broker executes calls; never fabricate successful observations. Existing
runtime supports arithmetic and selected length conversions only. SymPy can be an
offline verifier without becoming a serving tool. Broader science tools require
separate runtime changes and tests before tool-training examples are admitted.

D. Existing synthetic corpora, optional later
TinyGSM and OpenR1-Math can inform approaches or supply audited subsets after a
separate provenance/license/length review. They are not CBSE curriculum substitutes.
Long competition-style solutions are a poor default fit for the current 2K model.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09241
https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-r1/OpenR1-Math-220k
No bulk ingestion in the first pilot.

5. DATA SHAPE AND FULL REVIEW

Each example carries: immutable ID/hash, family/ancestor IDs, source kind and IDs,
concept/grade, split, messages or lesson text, answer schema, assumptions/units,
canonical task, tool policy/events, generator provenance, verifier/version/results,
review judgments, native tokenizer counts and assistant-target counts.
Source kinds: organic, codex_original, codex_source_grounded, programmatic,
programmatic_with_codex_wording. A generated paraphrase keeps original provenance.

Pipeline:
1. Acquire eligible originals; preserve hashes and source attribution.
2. Extract with equations, superscripts, tables and units intact. Check extracted
   text against page renders; reject unresolved OCR/equation corruption.
3. Canonicalize and assign family splits before generating variants.
4. Generate bounded batches; preserve raw outputs and reject/repair histories.
5. Verify every numerical answer and every step that can be checked mechanically.
6. Fully review each admitted explanation/dialogue; wrong intermediate teaching is
   a failure even if the last answer is right. Repair as a new immutable version.
7. Semantic science review against source anchors; unresolved claims quarantined.
8. Deduplicate text, expressions, templates and source ancestry across all splits.
9. Pack complete examples with correct masking; count native tokens and targets.
10. Freeze manifests, verify hashes, back up to GCS and publish aggregate reports.

Checks by discipline:
- Arithmetic: Fraction/Decimal or integers, signs, order of operations and rounding.
- Algebra/calculus: symbolic equivalence PLUS domains, excluded roots, endpoint
  conditions and constants. Numerical spot checks alone do not establish a proof.
- Geometry/proofs: explicit assumptions and step validity; diagram-free problems
  only initially. Cases needing expert adjudication remain unadmitted if unresolved.
- Physics: dimensions, units, sign conventions, limiting cases and physical bounds.
- Chemistry: atoms/charge conservation, stoichiometric ratios and conditions;
  balancing a reaction does not establish that the proposed reaction occurs.
- Biology: source-grounded causal links and exceptions, not exact-string grading.

Keep deliberate incorrect student work in user/context fields. Assistant targets
must identify and correct it; do not train a wrong answer as an unqualified response.

6. DATA VOLUMES, MIXTURES AND LENGTHS — PROPOSALS, NOT REQUIREMENTS

Pilot: 12,000 admitted training examples, excluding development/test and retention.
- 3,000 arithmetic/fractions/ratios/percentages.
- 2,500 basic algebra and word-problem translation.
- 1,500 measurement, units, estimation and basic geometry.
- 2,000 foundational science explanations/predictions.
- 1,500 numerical science problems with explicit assumptions.
- 1,500 tutoring/misconception/tool-and-recall sessions.

By provenance, a planning target is 20% eligible organic-derived examples,
40% original Codex examples and 40% programmatic tasks with reviewed wording.
These are intersecting provenance counts, not additional examples. If eligible
organic material is insufficient, reduce the pilot or revise the mix explicitly;
do not silently relabel generated prose as organic or bypass eligibility checks.

Length: primarily 128–1,024 native tokens; complete longer examples may use the
remaining 2K budget with reply headroom. No padding to 3K–4K. Long tutoring is a
later branch only after actual 4K hardware/data/quality qualification.
Estimate tokens using the real tokenizer. At 200–500 assistant targets/example,
12K examples would mean roughly 2.4–6M unique targets, before retention sampling;
measure actual values rather than treating this range as a budget commitment.

Later envelopes if each stage earns expansion:
Secondary: 40K–80K cumulative admitted examples.
Senior secondary: 100K–250K cumulative, balanced by concept and failure modes.
These are capacity-planning ranges, not a prediction of sufficient data or quality.
Review effort may dominate generation. Run a 200-example dry batch to measure
acceptance rate, review time and token cost before committing to these volumes.

7. TRAINING EXPERIMENTS

Keep architecture/tokenizer/context fixed initially. Two independent routes:
A. SFT-only from the best reviewed conversational checkpoint.
B. Educational continued pretraining from the selected base, THEN matched SFT.
Route B runs only if A exposes knowledge deficits and an eligible organic/lesson
corpus is ready. Do not launch a grid of base/epoch/LR combinations.

For A, start with 75% new school and 25% existing reviewed conversation/tool
assistant-target exposure. These are sampling proposals; adjust only on dev evidence.
For B, start with 60% organic education, 20% verified synthetic lessons and 20%
existing eligible general-language tokens. Educational pretraining uses text-token
loss; SFT uses assistant-target masking. Do not compare their token counts as equal.

Start at 2K, a bounded 1-pass SFT diagnostic; maximum second pass only if development
improves without retention failure. Select LR using previous own-model evidence and
a short stability pilot. No arbitrary production hyperparameters before profiling.
Candidate caps, checkpoints and phase deadlines must be written before launch.
No RL/preference stage until supervised examples and verifiable rewards work.
If two qualified attempts fail, stop and inspect data/representation/capacity instead
of increasing epochs. Preserve the incumbent and report failed hypotheses.

8. EVALUATION AND PROMOTION

Pilot: 300 development cases plus 500 fresh locked test cases, disjoint from 12K
training examples. Test allocation: 150 arithmetic, 100 algebra, 75 units/geometry,
75 conceptual science, 50 numerical science, 50 complete tutoring sessions.
Report sessions and turns separately. Avoid allowing multi-turn counts to inflate
single-question accuracy. Keep seeded/executable oracle answers private.

Separate tool-disabled and tool-enabled fresh runs; deterministic matched decoding,
context and reply budgets. Record correct problem setup, answer, units, explanation,
actual tool arguments/results, follow-up consistency, clarification and termination.
No MCQ-only headline. Include wrong-premise, ambiguous, impossible and changed-data
cases. Include 39+1 and72-19 as exposed regression cases, never unseen test evidence.

Leakage controls: disjoint family templates, parameters, text/source lineages and
reserved composition patterns, not just random row splits. Freeze before generation.
Tests used to guide a repair become dev; replenish fresh acceptance cases. Existing
base-corpus contamination cannot be ruled out for public exams; disclose this.

Proposed pilot promotion gates, assessed on fresh locked results:
- >=90% exact arithmetic, >=80% basic algebra/units/geometry, >=80% conceptual
  and numerical science with a reviewed correct explanation.
- >=95% tool-assisted numerical accuracy and no fabricated tool execution.
- >=80% complete tutoring-session success, with corrections remembered.
- All18 protected tool cases retained; preserve previously passing historical cases
  or explicitly reject promotion on regression. Improve broad conversation results,
  not merely the already-easy protected suite.
- Report numerators, denominators and uncertainty; small cells do not certify a
  topic. Bootstrap paired differences by problem family, not by correlated variants.
These are engineering gates, not claims of educational certification.

Before a Class XII claim: expand to fresh per-grade/per-subject coverage and at least
100 independently reviewed cases in each claimed major subject-grade bucket, with
numerical/conceptual/proof mix and chapter floors. Publish weak/uncovered chapters;
a strong pooled score cannot hide failure in organic chemistry or calculus.
